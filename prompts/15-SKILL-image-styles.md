# Nano Banana — stylized render & animation looks (Pixar-class 3D, anime, hand-drawn, 3D registers, game-engine)

> **STATUS: UNVALIDATED (drafted 2026-07-09, research pass only).** No field round has scored these registers yet — the only adjacent validated evidence is Round 14 (modern cel-shaded anime, PASS 20/22, `field_findings.md`). Run a validation round per family, score with the critique rubric in `references/composition_posing_and_critique.md`, then update or strike the blocks below.

This file deepens the one-line cue blocks in `references/graphic_and_illustration_styles.md` into full production registers. Same laws apply everywhere:

- **One register per image.** Mixing two ("Ghibli but Pixar-3D") cancels both.
- **Name the register precisely.** "Cartoon" / "anime" / "3D" alone average to mush. Name the sub-style and 3-4 concrete signatures.
- **Kill the photoreal stack.** These are the frames where the skill's realism machinery (skin pores, grain, halation, capture-medium tells) must be *dropped*, not just omitted — a Pixar frame with vellus hair or a Ghibli frame with 35mm grain is a register contamination. The Round 14 anime pass correctly carried NO photoreal texture; treat that as the rule.
- **Style words replace camera words only partially.** Lighting direction, shot size, and composition grammar (`references/framing_scene_grammar_and_continuity.md`) still work in every register — a stylized frame still needs a key light side and a shot size.
- **Named-studio caution.** Studio and film names (Pixar, Ghibli, Spider-Verse) work as style shortcuts but can trip refusals or drift on some surfaces. The robust form is *descriptive signatures first, name optional*: lead with the concrete cues and let the name reinforce, or drop the name entirely. Never render trademarked characters.

How these looks are actually made (useful because describing the *making* prompts better than naming the brand): feature-CG looks come from path-traced global illumination + subsurface scattering + physically-based shading with stylized geometry (the Pixar recipe is roughly 80% rendering craft, 20% character design); anime is cel-painted flats with hard shadow shapes over painted backgrounds; hand-drawn looks are pigment-on-paper physics (tooth, bleed, granulation); game-engine looks are real-time GI (Lumen-style) over ultra-dense geometry with a recognizable slightly-too-crisp finish.

## 1. Feature-animation 3D ("Pixar-class") registers

The family everyone asks for by studio name. Differentiate by **surface finish + proportion system + lighting philosophy**.

| Register | Cue block |
|---|---|
| **Pixar-soft** (the default ask) | polished 3D feature-animation render; soft rounded character forms, oversized expressive eyes with wet specular catchlights, simplified-but-appealing proportions; subsurface scattering on skin giving a warm translucent glow; soft global illumination with warm bounce fill; storytelling light — golden-hour key or gentle rim, teal-shadow/warm-highlight grade; smooth matte surfaces with restrained micro-texture; shallow cinematic DOF |
| **DreamWorks-broad** | sharper, more angular stylization than Pixar-soft; bolder squash-and-stretch poses, caricatured silhouettes, comedic expression pushed to the edge; punchier saturated palette; harder key light, less painterly bounce |
| **Illumination-graphic** | simple bold shapes, high-saturation flat-lit candy palette, minimal surface detail, toy-like cleanliness; characters read at thumbnail size; bright even lighting, few shadows |
| **Stylized-painterly 3D (Spider-Verse register)** | 3D forms finished like printed comic art — halftone dots and line hatching instead of soft gradients, visible ink outlines, chromatic-aberration misprint offsets, screen-tone shadows, graphic speed lines; deliberately broken physical rendering |
| **Painted-texture 3D (Arcane register)** | full deep block in §1b — do not improvise from this row |
| **Disney-Hyperion cinematic (Big Hero 6 register)** | full deep block in §1b — do not improvise from this row |
| **Stop-motion-look CG (Laika/Aardman register)** | see §4 claymation block — the puppet/miniature language lives there |

**Prompt skeleton (Pixar-soft):**
> *"A [subject] in polished 3D feature-animation style — soft rounded forms, oversized expressive eyes with bright specular catchlights, subsurface scattering giving skin a warm translucent glow. [Scene/action]. Soft global illumination, warm golden key from [side] with gentle teal shadow fill, shallow depth of field. Smooth matte render, vibrant but harmonious palette, emotional [beat] expression. No photographic grain, no realistic skin pores."*

**Notes:**
- Eyes carry the register. If eyes come out small/realistic, the frame reads "3D render", not "feature animation". Force: *oversized expressive eyes, large iris, wet specular catchlight*.
- SSS is the anti-plastic move *inside* stylization: without *subsurface scattering / translucent warm skin*, characters go waxy-dead.
- The teal-shadow/warm-highlight grade is a near-universal feature-CG signature — cheap and effective.
- For the viral **photo→Pixar identity conversion**, run it as a Framework 2/4 edit: *"Reimagine the person in the attached photo as a 3D feature-animation character — keep their identity, hairstyle, and outfit recognizable; rounded soft forms, oversized expressive eyes, warm SSS skin, soft GI lighting. Keep the pose and framing of the original."*

## 1b. Named-production deep blocks — SEPARATE files, never mix

Two productions asked for constantly, sitting at opposite poles of stylized 3D. Each has its **own standalone reference file** — go there for the full how-it's-made, lighting recipes, cue block, skeleton, and register police:

- **Arcane / Fortiche** (painted-texture hybrid 3D — light is hand-PAINTED onto textures) → `references/style_arcane_fortiche.md`
- **Physically-lit feature CG** (light fully SIMULATED — path-traced GI + SSS; canonical example: Big Hero 6-era Disney-Hyperion, used as inspiration only, never as a prompt word) → `references/style_feature_cg_simulated_light.md`

**They are incompatible registers.** Vocabulary from one contaminates the other — pick one file per frame, strike the other's words.

**Hard separation table:**

| Axis | Arcane / Fortiche | Feature CG, simulated light (ref: Big Hero 6) |
|---|---|---|
| Light | painted onto textures by hand | physically simulated GI, real multi-bounce |
| Surface | visible brushstrokes, grit | clean, smooth, polished |
| Background | 2D matte painting, concept-art | full 3D city, thousands of real light sources |
| FX | hand-drawn 2D, stepped 12 fps energy | simulated, smooth |
| Mood | moody, theatrical, adult | warm, optimistic, soft |
| Banned words | smooth, glossy, cute, soft GI | painterly, brushstroke, gritty, matte-painted |

## 2. Anime & manga registers

Never say just "anime". Route by sub-style:

| Register | Cue block |
|---|---|
| **Ghibli-watercolor** | hand-painted watercolor backgrounds with visible pigment washes and soft edges; gentle characters with clean simple linework and soft rounded faces; lush natural detail (grass, clouds, wind); calm pastoral mood; muted natural palette, diffuse daylight; painterly BG / flat-cel character contrast |
| **90s retro cel** | cel-painted limited palette, hand-inked heavy line weight, chunky angular hair highlights, film-grain and slight VHS color bleed, muted OAV-era colors, planar hard-edged shading, retro screencap framing with subtitle-safe headroom |
| **Modern digital cel (Round 14-adjacent — closest to validated)** | crisp digital linework, flat cel shading with hard two-tone shadow shapes, saturated clean palette, painterly detailed background, bloom-lit highlights, sharp 2K finish |
| **Shinkai-cinematic** | near-photographic painted urban/sky backgrounds, towering cumulus and stratus clouds, golden-hour god rays and lens-flare bloom, hyper-detailed light on ordinary objects (train crossings, convenience stores, power lines), small quiet figures in vast scenery, emotional two-shot or solo framing |
| **Shonen-action** | dynamic action pose mid-move, speed lines and impact frames, bold thick outlines, high-energy saturated colors, dramatic foreshortened limbs, gritted expression, camera tilted low for power |
| **Shoujo-soft** | delicate thin linework, slender elongated figures, enormous detailed eyes with layered highlights, pastel palette, floral motifs and sparkle overlays, soft vignette bloom |
| **Chibi / super-deformed** | 2-3 head-tall proportions, giant head and eyes, tiny simplified body and hands, thick clean outlines, flat bright colors, sticker-friendly silhouette |
| **Manga B&W** | pure black-and-white inked page, screentone halftone shading, dramatic hatching, panel-ready composition, speed lines, no color |

**Prompt skeleton (modern cel):**
> *"An anime illustration in modern digital cel style — crisp clean linework, flat cel shading with hard-edged two-tone shadows, [subject + action], detailed painterly background of [location], saturated harmonious palette, soft bloom on highlights. Flat 2D animation finish — no 3D render look, no photographic grain or texture."*

**Notes:**
- The #1 failure is drift toward 3D-CG or photoreal. Close every anime prompt with *flat 2D cel finish, no 3D render, no photo texture*.
- 90s-retro NEEDS its degradation cues (grain, muted palette, chunky highlights) — without them it renders as modern digital.
- Background register ≠ character register: Ghibli/Shinkai = painterly BG + flat character; state both halves.
- If a surface balks at "Ghibli"/"Miyazaki", swap to the descriptive block (*hand-painted watercolor animation backgrounds, gentle pastoral mood, clean soft character linework*) — it lands the same look.
- Manga pages with dialogue: quote the text per `references/text_rendering.md`; Pro for legible balloons.

## 2b. Western cartoon registers

| Register | Cue block |
|---|---|
| **Rubber-hose 1930s (Fleischer/Cuphead)** | vintage 1930s cel cartoon — noodle-limbed characters with no joints, pie-cut eyes, white gloves, bouncy rhythmic poses, inkblot black-and-white or muted early-Technicolor palette, film scratches and dust, hand-inked wobble in the linework, surreal gag energy |
| **Mid-century UPA / limited flat** | 1950s modernist limited animation — flat graphic shapes, off-register color blocks, angular stylized figures, textured paper background, jazzy limited palette, design-forward negative space |
| **Saturday-morning cel (60s–80s)** | flat cel colors over painted backgrounds, thick uniform outlines, simple repeatable character designs, slightly faded broadcast palette, cel dust and shadow of the cel layer |
| **Modern flat TV cartoon (CalArts/Adventure-Time adjacent)** | thin clean outlines, simple bean-shaped characters with dot-and-line faces, flat bright candy palette, minimal shading, rounded geometry, pastel painted backgrounds |
| **Prime-time adult cartoon** | bold uniform outlines, flat colors with minimal gradient shading, standardized character-sheet proportions, sitcom staging, even lighting |
| **Newspaper comic strip** | loose expressive pen linework, minimal or hatched shading, limited flat color or B&W, hand-lettered caption space, four-panel-friendly simplicity |

**Notes:** the register lives in the *era's production tells* — rubber-hose needs film wear + pie-cut eyes; UPA needs off-register print misalignment; modern-flat needs the bean silhouette. Without the era tells everything collapses into "generic cartoon". Same 2D closers as anime: *flat 2D cel finish, no 3D render*.

## 3. Hand-drawn & traditional-media registers

The register lives in the **medium physics**: tool + paper + stroke behavior. Always name the paper.

| Register | Cue block |
|---|---|
| **Graphite pencil sketch** | loose confident graphite lines, varied pressure, visible construction strokes and eraser ghosts, smudged tonal shading, white cartridge paper with slight tooth |
| **Charcoal** | bold charred blacks and soft grey smudges, rough expressive strokes, dramatic tonal contrast, fingertip blending, grainy charcoal paper texture |
| **Pen & ink / crosshatch** | tapered dip-pen ink lines, crosshatched and stippled shadow build-up, confident contour lines, pure black on off-white, no grey washes |
| **Ink + watercolor wash** | clean ink outlines with loose translucent watercolor washes bleeding past the lines, cold-press paper texture, granulation in the pigment pools, white paper breathing through |
| **Watercolor storybook** | soft pencil under-drawing, layered translucent washes, bleeding wet-on-wet edges, pastel palette, generous white space, gentle children's-book warmth |
| **Gouache storybook** | matte opaque paint, flat confident shapes with visible brush edges, warm mid-century palette, slight paper texture, no gloss no gradient |
| **Colored pencil** | visible directional pencil strokes, waxy layered color build-up, paper tooth showing through lights, hand-hatched shading |
| **Children's-book whimsy** | innocent rounded characters, wobbly charming linework, limited friendly palette, storybook composition with room for text, no harsh lines, no photorealism |
| **Vintage etching / engraving** | fine parallel engraved lines, dense crosshatch tone, aged paper, scientific-plate or banknote character |

**Prompt skeleton (ink + watercolor):**
> *"A hand-drawn illustration of [subject] — clean tapered ink outlines with loose translucent watercolor washes, edges bleeding softly past the lines, granulation pooling in the shadows, cold-press watercolor paper texture visible throughout, generous white space. Flat scanned-artwork finish, evenly lit — no digital glow, no 3D shading, no photograph."*

**Notes:**
- **The digital-glow tell** is this family's plastic-skin: AI hand-drawn output tends to a backlit airbrushed sheen. Kill with *flat scanned-artwork finish, no digital glow, no airbrush gradient* + a named paper (*cold-press watercolor paper, grainy charcoal paper, yellowed parchment*).
- Imperfection is the realism layer here: *visible construction lines, uneven wash edges, pigment granulation, eraser ghosts* do the job micro-imperfections do in photoreal.
- "Sketch" alone gives a finished rendering with sketch lines on top. Force looseness: *unfinished, loose gesture, visible construction strokes, parts left undrawn*.

## 3b. Fine-art & print registers

| Register | Cue block |
|---|---|
| **Oil painting (classical)** | thick layered oil on canvas, visible impasto brushwork, glazed shadow depth, canvas weave texture, gallery varnish sheen, chiaroscuro single-source light |
| **Impressionist** | loose broken brushstrokes, color mixed optically on the canvas, fleeting natural light, vibrating complementary dabs, edges dissolving, plein-air subject |
| **Ukiyo-e woodblock** | flat color planes, bold carved outlines, visible woodgrain in flat inks, limited mineral palette, stylized waves/clouds/mountains, vertical composition with seal and calligraphy space |
| **Art nouveau** | sinuous whiplash linework, ornamental floral borders, flat decorative color inside strong contours, gilded accents, elegant elongated figures, poster-lithograph finish |
| **Moebius / ligne claire sci-fi** | uniform-weight clear ink line, no hatching, flat luminous color fields, vast surreal desert-tech landscapes, tiny figures in monumental space, precise geometric detail, dreamlike stillness |
| **American comic book** | inked outlines with muscular figure drawing, dynamic panel-ready action, Ben-Day/halftone print dots, four-color print palette with slight mis-registration, dramatic letterbox captions |
| **Pop art** | advertising-derived bold graphics, hard black outlines, flat saturated primaries, halftone dots at visible scale, repeated-panel or single iconic subject |
| **Vintage travel poster / lithograph** | simplified heroic landscape shapes, flat gradient skies, limited spot-color litho inks, retro display typography space, aged paper |

**Notes:** pair every movement with a **medium phrase** — "impressionism" alone drifts; *"impressionist oil painting, loose broken brushstrokes, canvas texture"* holds. For print registers (ukiyo-e, comic, pop, litho) the paper/print physics ARE the register: name the dot pattern, registration error, ink flatness, paper age. Artist names: prefer the descriptive block; long-dead masters (Monet, Hokusai, Mucha) are safe reinforcement, living artists' names are not.

## 4. 3D-render registers beyond feature animation

| Register | Cue block |
|---|---|
| **Clay render (studio-neutral)** | matte monochrome clay material, soft studio GI, gentle AO in the creases, no textures, sculpt-review aesthetic |
| **Claymation / stop-motion** | plasticine characters with visible fingerprints and thumb-dents, handcrafted slightly-crooked miniature set, warm tactile practical lighting, real-material imperfection everywhere; ban the words smooth/perfect/polished |
| **Toy / blind-box collectible ("figurine")** | glossy vinyl designer-toy figure, simplified chibi-adjacent proportions, visible seam lines and matte/gloss material mix, product-shot lighting on seamless sweep, in-scale packaging or acrylic base optional; the viral photo→figurine conversion is this register run as a Framework 2 edit (*"turn the person in the photo into a 1/7-scale collectible figurine on a desk, with its box art behind it"*) |
| **Soft-vinyl / squishy 3D** | rounded inflated forms, soft matte-rubber material with subtle SSS, pastel palette, gentle studio dome light — the friendly mascot/app-icon register |
| **Octane/product-CG hero** | hyper-clean physically-based render, controlled studio HDRI reflections, perfect materials (brushed metal, frosted glass, liquid), dark or gradient sweep, volumetric accent — the tech-ad register (pair with `references/commercial_framing_and_set_design.md`) |
| **Isometric miniature diorama** | true isometric camera (no perspective convergence), neat miniature-world clarity, soft even light, tilt-shift toy feel |
| **Low-poly** | faceted triangulated geometry, flat-shaded facets, limited palette, gradient sky lighting |
| **Voxel** | cubic voxel construction, hard pixel-cube edges, playful game-world palette |

**Notes:**
- Claymation's whole register is *evidence of hands*: fingerprints, tool marks, slightly-wrong set geometry. Without them it collapses into generic 3D.
- Figurine prompts benefit from the *context of realness*: desk, box, scale reference, acrylic stand — the figure is photoreal-as-object even though the character is stylized. This is the one register where photoreal capture language (soft product lighting, shallow DOF) SHOULD stay.
- Route figurine/collectible conversions through Pro when box art carries text.

## 5. Game-engine registers ("Unreal Engine look")

"Unreal Engine" in a prompt is a *quality-and-finish* signal, not a literal engine. It pulls: ultra-detailed geometry, real-time GI look, crisp specular, volumetric light, slightly-too-clean surfaces. Route by intent:

| Register | Cue block |
|---|---|
| **UE5 cinematic still** | real-time cinematic render aesthetic — ultra-dense geometric detail, dynamic global illumination with soft infinite-bounce fill, volumetric god rays and atmospheric fog, crisp specular highlights, filmic tonemapping, shallow cinematic DOF; the polished game-trailer frame |
| **In-game screenshot** | third-person or first-person gameplay framing, slightly video-game-crisp materials, ambient-occlusion-heavy contact shadows, bloom on emissives, subtle HUD-free screenshot cleanliness, photo-mode composition |
| **Digital-human close-up (MetaHuman register)** | high-fidelity real-time digital human — accurate skin shader with pore-level normal detail, strand-based grooms, but the telltale real-time finish: slightly-too-even specular, clean uncanny perfection; use only when the brief WANTS the digital-human look — for a real human face go to `references/frame_realism_engine.md` instead |
| **ArchViz real-time** | unbuilt-architecture visualization — physically-plausible sun study, glossy clean materials, staged furniture, aspirational dusk or clear-noon light, slightly idealized cleanliness (pair with the architecture recipes in `references/campaign_and_specialist_genres.md`) |
| **Stylized game-art** | hand-painted-texture game assets, exaggerated chunky proportions, saturated fantasy palette, painterly specular — the MMO/hero-shooter concept register |

**Prompt skeleton (UE5 cinematic):**
> *"A cinematic real-time render of [subject/environment] — ultra-detailed geometry, dynamic global illumination with soft bounced fill, volumetric light shafts through [atmosphere], crisp specular highlights, filmic tonemapping, shallow depth of field. Polished game-cinematic finish."*

**Notes:**
- Decide **game-render vs photograph** and commit. The engine look's value IS its slight hyper-clean unreality; if the brief wants "indistinguishable from a photo", this is the wrong file — go to `references/frame_realism_engine.md`.
- "Unreal Engine 5, Octane render, 8K" keyword-salad stacking is legacy diffusion habit — on Nano Banana, one engine register + a described scene beats the pile.
- Environment > people in this family: engine-register humans trend uncanny. For characters, prefer §1 stylized or full photoreal.

## 6. Style-transfer & conversion workflows (all families)

- **Photo → any register above:** Framework 4. *"Recreate the exact subject and composition of the attached photo in [register block] — preserving identity, pose, and framing."* Always restate what to preserve; style transfer eats faces otherwise.
- **Style-consistency across a set:** generate the hero in the register, then feed it back as a style reference for every subsequent frame (*"match the exact rendering style, palette, and line quality of the attached image"*). Same logic as the identity lock in `references/identity_lock_and_filmmaking.md`, applied to style.
- **Register + text:** posters/covers in any register — quote the text and name the font per `references/text_rendering.md`; render 2K+; Pro for heavy type.
- **Iteration routing:** iterate registers on Nano Banana 2 (cheap, fast — style registers survive low res better than realism does), finish on Pro at 2K/4K.

## 7. Failure modes

| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Pixar style" comes out photoreal-human with big eyes (uncanny) | lead with *3D feature-animation render, soft rounded stylized forms* before any human description; ban pores/grain |
| Feature-CG character looks waxy/dead | add *subsurface scattering, warm translucent skin, wet specular eye catchlights* |
| Anime drifts to 3D-CG | close with *flat 2D cel finish, no 3D render, no CGI* |
| Anime looks generic/modern when 90s was asked | add the degradation stack: *cel grain, muted OAV palette, chunky hair highlights, heavy line weight* |
| Hand-drawn output has airbrushed digital glow | *flat scanned-artwork finish, no digital glow* + named paper texture |
| Watercolor renders as photo-with-filter | name medium physics: *translucent washes, bleeding wet edges, granulation, cold-press paper tooth* |
| Claymation looks like clean 3D | *visible fingerprints, thumb-dents, handcrafted crooked set*; ban smooth/perfect/polished |
| Figurine floats in a void, reads as plain render | ground it: desk surface, box art, acrylic base, soft product lighting, scale context |
| "Unreal Engine" gives a generic shiny render | pick ONE §5 register and describe the scene; drop the 8K keyword pile |
| Two registers muddled (Ghibli-Pixar soup) | one register per image — restate the single register and strike the other's vocabulary |

# Nano Banana — fine-art movement deep registers

> **STATUS: UNVALIDATED (researched 2026-07-09, agent research round).** Extends `stylized_render_and_animation_looks.md` §3b (classical oil, impressionism, ukiyo-e, art nouveau, Moebius, American comic, pop art, travel poster live there).
>
> **Skeleton for every movement prompt:** `[movement + date/artist anchor] + [physical medium + support] + [light behavior] + [palette clamp] + [brush/print mechanics] + [composition rule] + [one negative guarding the known failure]`. Date anchors ("1907", "Song dynasty", "1938") disambiguate better than adjectives.
>
> **Two universal failure classes:** (1) *realism creep* — the model re-imposes photographic light/shadows/perspective on flat traditions (Byzantine, miniature, manuscript, shan shui, Cubism): always negate depth and shadows explicitly. (2) *register collapse* — painterly movements slide into trending digital art (Pre-Raphaelite→fantasy, Romanticism→matte painting, Fauvism→colorful impressionism): anchor with medium physics and ban glow/bloom.

## 1. Baroque tenebrism (Caravaggio register)
Single hard raking source; figures emerging from near-black void; umber/sienna/blood-red flesh palette; theatrical frozen gesture at the dramatic peak; hyperreal flesh in the light pool; crisp shadow terminators.
**Cue:** *Baroque oil painting, Caravaggio-style dramatic tenebrism — single hard raking light from upper left, figures emerging from a near-black void, deep umber and blood-red palette, hyperreal flesh modeling in oil glazes, theatrical frozen gesture, crisp shadow terminator, dark ground canvas, no ambient fill light*
**Fail→fix:** contrast softens to moody-photo → *"background pure darkness, no fill light, no visible environment"* + name one diegetic source (candle, window shaft).

## 2. Dutch Golden Age (Vermeer / vanitas register)
Cool soft daylight from a single left leaded window; pointillé impasto highlight dots on pearls/glass/bread; ultramarine + lemon-yellow + plaster grey; invisible smooth brushwork, thin glazes; psychological hush, figures absorbed in tasks. Vanitas grammar: skull, snuffed candle, tipped glass, timepiece, lemon-peel spiral.
**Cue:** *Dutch Golden Age oil painting — Vermeer-style soft daylight through a leaded window at left, quiet domestic interior, ultramarine and lemon-yellow accents against plaster grey, invisible smooth brushwork, thin oil glazes, pointillé specular highlights on glass and pearls, camera-obscura optical realism, hushed stillness* (vanitas variant: *still life with skull, snuffed candle, tipped glass, half-peeled lemon, pocket watch, dark ground*)
**Fail→fix:** window light renders golden-hour warm → *"cool north daylight, silvery diffuse light, no direct sunbeams."*

## 3. Romanticism sublime (Turner / Friedrich registers — pick one)
Tiny human vs colossal nature. **Turner:** forms dissolving into molten golden light, vortex compositions, scumbled translucent veils. **Friedrich:** Rückenfigur from behind, fog-layered vistas, cold violet-blue dawn.
**Cue (Friedrich):** *Romantic sublime landscape oil — lone figure seen from behind on a cliff edge, vast fog-layered mountain vista, cold violet-blue dawn light, overwhelming scale of nature, atmospheric mist bands, 19th-century German Romanticism* · **Cue (Turner):** *forms dissolving into molten golden light, vortex of storm and sea spray, scumbled translucent oil veils, luminous white core, tiny ship swallowed by the sublime*
**Fail→fix:** crisp fantasy matte painting → *"dissolving edges, no sharp detail in distance, painted atmosphere, visible scumbled brushwork"* — drop "epic/detailed."

## 4. Pre-Raphaelite
Jewel-saturated color luminous from the wet-white-ground technique; uniform hyper-detail everywhere, no atmospheric blur; red/copper-haired pale melancholic women; medieval/Arthurian subjects; figures embedded in botanically-accurate dense nature; flat even daylight.
**Cue:** *Pre-Raphaelite oil painting, 1850s Brotherhood style — red-haired woman in flowing medieval gown, jewel-toned saturated color on wet white ground, every leaf and petal in equal botanical hyper-detail, symbolic flowers, flat even daylight, no atmospheric blur, dense riverbank nature, melancholic heavy-lidded gaze*
**Fail→fix:** generic fantasy-princess glow → *"uniform sharp focus foreground to background, botanical accuracy, no bloom, Victorian oil painting."*

## 5. Klimt golden phase (Symbolism register)
Oil + real gold/silver leaf; naturalistic soft face and hands embedded in flat mosaic ornament; spirals, checkerboards, concentric circles; vertical frieze compositions; gold dominant with crimson/emerald/violet accents.
**Cue:** *Gustav Klimt Golden Phase painting, Vienna Secession 1907 — oil and gold leaf on canvas, softly modeled realistic face emerging from flat ornamental gold ground, mosaic pattern fields of spirals checkerboards and concentric circles, matte and burnished gold interplay, vertical frieze composition, crimson and emerald jewel accents*
**Fail→fix:** everything gold-tints into bronze-statue → *"naturalistic pale skin tones, gold confined to garments and background."*

## 6. Expressionism (Munch / Kirchner registers)
Distorted elongated figures, perspective bent by emotion; acidic clashing color; raw visible brushwork (long streaky Munch strokes / jagged angular Kirchner hatching); mask-like anxious faces; claustrophobic tilted compositions; woodcut variant with gouged blacks.
**Cue:** *German Expressionist oil painting, Munch style — swirling streaky brushwork, undulating blood-orange sky, elongated distorted figure with mask-like anxious face, warped perspective bending with emotion, raw unblended color, psychological dread* (Kirchner variant: *jagged angular strokes, acidic pink and poison-green city street, tilted claustrophobic perspective*)
**Fail→fix:** model "fixes" the anatomy → *"deliberately crude drawing, warped anatomy, no realistic proportions."*

## 7. Cubism (analytic / synthetic)
Subject shattered into faceted planes from multiple viewpoints at once; analytic = near-monochrome ochre/grey overlapping translucent planes; synthetic = flatter brighter shapes + collage (newsprint, faux wood grain, stenciled letters); no single light source.
**Cue:** *Analytic Cubist oil painting, 1911 style — portrait fragmented into overlapping translucent geometric facets, multiple viewpoints simultaneously, face in profile and frontal at once, muted ochre-grey-brown monochrome, shallow picture plane, each plane lit independently* (synthetic variant: *flat bold shapes, collaged newsprint and faux wood grain, stenciled lettering, guitar and bottle still life*)
**Fail→fix:** outputs low-poly 3D → *"flat painted planes, no 3D rendering, multiple inconsistent viewpoints in one figure, shallow flattened space"*; forbid "low poly."

## 8. Surrealism (Dalí / Magritte registers)
Old-master-precise smooth oil applied to impossible dream content. **Dalí:** barren plains, infinite horizons, long hard shadows, melting forms, double images. **Magritte:** bowler hats, cloud skies, wrong-scale objects, deadpan flat daylight.
**Cue (Magritte):** *Surrealist oil painting, Belgian 1930s style — deadpan flat daylight, bowler-hatted man, ordinary objects at impossible scale, blue sky with white cumulus, smooth invisible academic brushwork, uncanny juxtaposition rendered with documentary precision* · **Cue (Dalí):** *vast barren dream plain, infinite horizon, long hard raking shadows, melting soft forms propped on crutches, hyperreal old-master oil finish*
**Fail→fix:** "surreal" triggers psychedelic swirls → never use the word alone; pair with *"precise academic oil realism, calm composition, no psychedelic patterns."*

## 9. Abstract Expressionism (Pollock / Rothko registers)
**Pollock:** all-over drip lattice, layered enamel skeins, no focal point, raw canvas between layers. **Rothko:** 2–3 stacked soft-edged rectangles, thin stained pigment glowing from within, hazy breathing edges. Paint-as-event; no subject, no light, no space.
**Cue (Pollock):** *Abstract Expressionist action painting, 1950 New York School — all-over drip lattice of layered enamel skeins in black white silver and ochre, no focal point, gestural energy recorded in paint, raw canvas visible between layers* · **Cue (Rothko):** *color field painting — three stacked soft-edged rectangles, maroon over orange on deep red ground, thin stained pigment glowing from within, hazy breathing edges*
**Fail→fix:** representational content sneaks in / drips become tidy vector splashes → *"purely non-representational, no recognizable objects, physical paint texture, irregular organic drips"*; for Rothko ban hard edges.

## 10. Fauvism (Matisse / Derain register)
Arbitrary non-natural color (red trees, green face-shadows, pink skies); pure unmixed saturated pigment in broad flat strokes; raw canvas breathing between patches; bold blue/green contours; flattened space; high-key, no true darks.
**Cue:** *Fauvist oil painting, 1905 style — arbitrary non-natural color, red trees, pink sky, green shadows on skin, pure unmixed saturated pigment in broad flat strokes, raw canvas showing between patches, bold blue contour lines, flattened space, Mediterranean harbor, joyful high-key palette, no tonal shading*
**Fail→fix:** collapses to colorful impressionism → name the color inversions explicitly (*"crimson tree canopy, viridian face shadow"*) — the model won't invent wrong-color assignments on its own.

## 11. Rococo (Fragonard register)
Pastel confection palette (powder blue, blush, mint, champagne gold); soft diffuse glow, no hard shadows; frothy asymmetric S-curves, whipped-cream foliage; aristocratic play (swings, garden trysts, cherubs); feathery flickering brushwork; porcelain rosy skin; rocaille ornament.
**Cue:** *Rococo oil painting, 1760s French court style — aristocratic garden scene with a swing, powder-blue and blush-pink pastel palette, soft diffuse glowing light with no hard shadows, frothy billowing foliage in feathery flickering brushstrokes, cascading silk dress, porcelain skin, playful S-curve composition, cherubs and rocaille ornament*
**Fail→fix:** drifts Victorian/princess-fantasy → pin century + palette: *"18th-century French court, panniers and powdered hair, pastel high-key, no dark tones, fête galante."*

## 12. Byzantine icon
Egg tempera on gessoed panel over burnished gold leaf; frontal symmetrical elongated figures, almond eyes, olive-shadowed faces built dark-to-light with white assist striations; inverse/flat perspective; incised gold halos; Greek inscriptions; craquelure and candle-smoke patina.
**Cue:** *Byzantine icon, egg tempera and burnished gold leaf on wood panel, 12th-century Constantinople — frontal symmetrical elongated figure, almond eyes and small mouth, olive-toned face built from dark base to fine white highlight lines, flat inverse perspective, incised gold halo, hard graphic drapery folds with gold striations, craquelure and candle-smoke patina*
**Fail→fix:** modern photoreal face modeling breaks sacred flatness → *"flat stylized face, no photorealism, no soft shading, medieval anatomical stylization, tempera on panel."*

## 13. Medieval illuminated manuscript
Mineral pigments (lapis, vermilion, verdigris) + raised burnished gold on vellum; PAGE-as-composition — text block in Gothic script, historiated initial, framed miniature, drollery-filled ivy borders; flat perspective, gold-diaper backgrounds; ruling lines, cockling, aged edges.
**Cue:** *Medieval illuminated manuscript page, 13th-century Gothic scriptorium — mineral pigment and raised burnished gold leaf on vellum, historiated initial capital, framed miniature with flat gold-diaper background, lapis blue and vermilion palette, ivy-scroll border with drollery creatures in the margins, blackletter text block, outlined figures with minimal modeling, visible ruling lines and vellum grain*
**Fail→fix:** framed fantasy painting with parchment filter → demand the full page architecture: *"complete manuscript page layout — text block plus miniature plus decorated border."* Script will garble: embrace as asemic or overlay real text in post.

## 14. Chinese ink-wash / shan shui
Carbon ink on absorbent xuan paper, no correction possible; graded washes ("five colors of ink") from bone-dry charcoal to watery pale; unpainted paper (liúbái) reads as mist/river/void; three stacked depth registers, not perspective; cun texture strokes on rock; tiny human accents; calligraphy + red seal.
**Cue:** *traditional Chinese shan shui ink-wash painting, Song-dynasty literati style — carbon ink on absorbent xuan paper, graded wet washes from deep black to pale watery grey, vast unpainted negative space as mist and river, layered mountain peaks in three stacked depth registers, dry axe-cut texture strokes on rock, tiny fisherman in a boat dwarfed by the landscape, ink bloom on damp paper, vertical hanging scroll, calligraphy inscription and red seal*
**Fail→fix:** sky gets filled, Western atmospheric color appears → *"monochrome ink only, no color, at least half the composition left as blank paper"* — quantify the emptiness.

## 15. Persian / Mughal miniature
Opaque gouache + gold on polished paper, burnished jewel-flat finish; flat tilted aerial perspective, scenes stacked vertically, no vanishing point, NO cast shadows; lapis/malachite/saffron/vermilion + gold skies; microscopic uniform detail edge to edge; ruled illuminated border (hashiya).
**Cue:** *Persian miniature painting, Safavid Shahnameh manuscript style — opaque gouache and gold leaf on polished paper, flat tilted aerial perspective with no vanishing point and no cast shadows, jewel-toned lapis malachite saffron and vermilion, gold sky, court garden scene with flowering trees, every carpet and textile pattern in microscopic uniform detail, elaborate ruled illuminated border* (Mughal variant: *Akbar-period, Persian flatness fused with Indian naturalism, delicate portrait likeness, hunt scene*)
**Fail→fix:** Western depth creeps in → explicitly negate: *"no shadows, no perspective depth, flat stacked composition, tilted ground plane."*

## 16. Soviet Constructivism (Rodchenko / Lissitzky register)
Lithograph poster: flat red/black/cream spot inks; aggressive 30–45° diagonal axis; halftone photomontage cutouts (worker, shouting profile, machinery) against geometric fields; bold circle/wedge armature; blocky angled sans-serif type as graphic object.
**Cue:** *Soviet Constructivist propaganda poster, 1924 Rodchenko style — lithograph in flat red black and cream spot colors, aggressive diagonal composition, halftone photomontage of a worker's shouting profile, bold geometric circle and wedge armature, blocky angled sans-serif typography integrated into the design, no gradients, cheap paper texture*
**Fail→fix:** drifts to painterly Socialist Realism (wrong movement) → *"photomontage and flat graphic shapes only, no painted realism"*; clamp palette to red/black/cream.

## 17. WPA poster (Federal Art Project register)
Hand-cut stencil silkscreen, 4–8 flat opaque ink layers; simplified monumental landscape shapes, one iconic focal element; muted-warm palette (teal, ochre, brick, forest green, cream); stepped-band skies; bold sans-serif title block; registration offset, toothy paper.
**Cue:** *WPA Federal Art Project silkscreen travel poster, 1938 — flat opaque ink layers in a limited five-color palette of teal ochre brick red and cream, hard-edged simplified landscape shapes, one monumental focal element, stepped-band sky, bold geometric sans-serif title block at bottom, slight screenprint registration offset, toothy paper texture, national-parks style*
**Fail→fix:** too-clean Dribbble-flat → cue the physical process: *"hand-cut stencil, visible ink texture, imperfect registration, maximum five colors."*

## Disambiguation notes
- **Gold-leaf trio:** Klimt = realistic face inside flat gold ornament · Byzantine = fully flat sacred figure on gold void · Persian/Mughal = gold as sky/accent inside a jewel-detailed flat scene.
- **Poster trio:** Constructivism = photomontage + diagonal + red/black · WPA = silkscreen landscape flats · vintage-travel (§3b core) = lithographic Deco elegance.

## 2. Toon-3D open world (BotW register)
**Made:** toon shader quantizing light into 2–3 soft bands + painterly gradient textures, hybridized with realistic atmospherics (volumetric light, fog, gouache skies).
**Looks:** soft-ramp two/three-band cel; watercolor environment palette; minimal/no outlines — forms separated by color; painterly clouds and god rays; grass as broad color fields; pastel distance fog; simplified flat-color faces.
**Cue:** *cel-shaded 3D adventure game art — soft toon shading with two-tone light bands, watercolor-inspired open-world landscape, rolling green fields, hazy pastel atmospheric perspective, painterly clouds and god rays, no outlines, gouache texture feel, 3D rendered game-engine look*
**Fail→fix:** collapses to flat 2D anime illustration → anchor *"3D rendered, game engine screenshot, toon shader"* to keep dimensional volume.

## 3. Anime-3D (Genshin register)
**Made:** anime-styled 3D models with custom toon shaders — hard shadow cutoffs hand-tuned per face, anime specular hair, thin outlines, glossy emissive FX.
**Looks:** hard-edge cel shadows with clean face shading (no realistic nose shadows); glossy ribbon hair highlights; large luminous eyes; thin dark outlines; ornate layered costume with elemental motifs; candy-saturated palette, glowing particles; bright skylit environments, soft bloom.
**Cue:** *anime-style 3D character render — cel-shaded with crisp shadow edges, glossy anime hair highlights, large expressive eyes, thin outline shader, ornate fantasy costume with glowing elemental accents, vibrant saturated colors, soft bloom, anime-3D hybrid gacha key-art quality*
**Fail→fix:** realistic skin shading breaks the anime face → *"flat anime face shading, no realistic facial shadows, clean cel face"*; ban "photorealistic skin, semi-realism."

## 4. Stylized realism (Fortnite register)
**Made:** exaggerated chunky sculpts + fully realistic PBR materials in Unreal with cinematic light — cartoon proportions, real surfaces, distance readability.
**Looks:** slightly oversized heads/hands, chunky simplified gear; real fabric weave and metal roughness on cartoon shapes; punchy color-blocked outfits; soft cinematic sky ambient + mild bloom; smooth simplified surfaces, no grit; confident readable silhouettes.
**Cue:** *stylized-realistic 3D game character, engine render — exaggerated cartoon proportions with realistic PBR materials, chunky readable silhouette, bright saturated color-blocked outfit, soft cinematic lighting, clean smooth surfaces, playful heroic pose, battle-royale key art*
**Fail→fix:** averages into Pixar-soft baby-face → *"game character, athletic adult proportions, battle-ready gear, video game key art render, not animated film still."*

## 5. Hero-shooter look (Overwatch/Valorant register)
**Made:** stylized PBR under strict art direction — "illustrative design": hand-painted texture accents, narrowed value range, geometric simplification, graphic flat-lit environments so characters pop.
**Looks:** per-character shape language, instantly readable distinct silhouettes; semi-real PBR + hand-painted gradient accents and clean decals; 2–3 hero colors vs desaturated graphic environment; crisp hard-surface tech (white/orange sci-fi armor + glow, or matte tactical + bold trims); soft studio-like key, clean AO, minimal grime.
**Cue:** *hero shooter character art, stylized semi-realistic 3D render — strong distinctive silhouette, clean PBR materials with hand-painted accents, limited bold color palette, glowing tech details, soft filmic lighting, simplified graphic background, confident three-quarter agent-reveal pose*
**Fail→fix:** greeble/strap overdetail destroys silhouette → *"clean simplified design, large readable shapes, minimal surface detail"*; ban "cluttered, busy, gritty."

## 6. Souls-like grim concept art (Dark Souls/Elden Ring register)
**Made:** painterly digital concept + photo-bash, drawing on Berserk, Bosch/Bruegel, Gothic/Romantic painting — beauty and rot together in muted values.
**Looks:** desaturated near-monochrome + one sickly accent (gold haze, ember, pale moonlight); ruined colossal Gothic architecture dwarfing tiny figures; ornate weathered armor, massive swords, grotesque flesh-armor bosses; heavy fog, dust, god rays through decay; dry-brush grit, no clean edges; melancholy grandeur.
**Cue:** *dark fantasy concept art, grim painterly rendering — desaturated muted palette with single ember-gold accent, colossal ruined gothic cathedral, lone knight in ornate weathered armor dwarfed by scale, heavy fog and god rays, textured dry-brush strokes, melancholy epic atmosphere*
**Fail→fix:** bright generic epic fantasy → lock the grade: *"overcast, desaturated, oppressive, low-key values"*; ban "vibrant, clear blue sky, cheerful."

## 7. 16-bit pixel key art (SNES JRPG register)
**Made:** hand-placed pixels on a strict grid, 16–32-color palettes, hue-shifted ramps, hand dithering, single-pixel highlights — lush Chrono Trigger/FF6 painterly pixel scenes.
**Looks:** consistent square pixel grid (never mixed sizes); cohesive limited palette, shadows hue-shift toward purple/blue not black; ordered dithering in skies; selective outlines (selout); layered parallax backgrounds; 4:3-era framing.
**Cue:** *16-bit pixel art, SNES JRPG key art scene — limited 32-color palette, hue-shifted shadows, hand-dithered gradient sunset sky, layered parallax background, crisp uniform pixel grid, sprite characters with selective outlines, golden-era JRPG pixel aesthetic*
**Fail→fix:** mixed pixel sizes ("fake pixel art") → *"uniform pixel grid, consistent pixel size throughout, true low resolution upscaled"*; ideally downscale/re-quantize in post.

## 8. PS2-era aesthetic
**Made:** low-poly models (~500–5k tris), ~256px textures, vertex lighting, heavy fog, early bloom — sixth-gen (Silent Hill 2 / San Andreas) now a nostalgia register.
**Looks:** faceted geometry, angular hands, polygonal faces; blurry stretched textures with baked-on detail; blocky smeared vertex-light transitions; heavy fog, short draw distance; early bloom, CRT/interlace softness; muted early-2000s grade.
**Cue:** *PS2-era video game graphics, early-2000s 3D render — low-poly character with visible polygon edges, blurry low-resolution textures, vertex lighting with blocky shading, heavy distance fog, muted dark grade, subtle bloom, CRT softness, sixth-generation console screenshot*
**Fail→fix:** model modernizes the render → pile on period constraints (*"low fidelity, jagged edges, no anti-aliasing, 480i screenshot"*); ban "high detail, ray tracing, modern render."

## 9. N64 low-poly
**Made:** <~800-tri characters from primitives, Gouraud vertex shading, tiny textures smeared by 3-point bilinear filtering, bright flat vertex colors — cheerier and blurrier than PS1.
**Looks:** primitives barely disguised as characters; signature smeary 3-point texture blur; smooth vertex-shaded gradients; saturated primaries; simple gradient skies, billboard foliage; chunky rounded proportions, mitten hands.
**Cue:** *N64 low-poly video game render, 1990s retro 3D — character built from simple geometric primitives, smooth gouraud vertex shading, blurry smeared 3-point filtered textures, bright saturated primary colors, simple gradient sky, chunky rounded cartoon proportions, 64-bit platformer screenshot*
**Fail→fix:** confused with modern faceted "lowpoly art" → *"retro game console screenshot, smooth-shaded low poly with blurry textures, NOT faceted flat-shaded modern lowpoly."*

## 10. Ink-and-jewel painted look (Hades/Supergiant register)
**Made:** pen-and-ink comic technique — bold inked lines and Mignola-style chiaroscuro + vintage-poster shapes, filled with saturated jewel tones, rim-lit against arenas falling into black; portraits lushly painted over the ink armature.
**Looks:** bold confident black ink, angular graphic shapes; deep pooled blacks swallowing scene edges; crimson/magenta/teal/gold glowing against black; strong colored rim light; flat Greek-motif ornament mixing with painted volume; glamorous sharp portraits.
**Cue:** *stylized game character portrait, bold ink linework with painted color — Mignola-inspired chiaroscuro, deep black shadows swallowing the scene edges, saturated jewel-tone crimson magenta and gold, glowing colored rim light, mythological ornament, vintage-poster graphic shapes*
**Fail→fix:** soft full-painterly fantasy, no ink structure → *"bold black inked outlines, hard-edged graphic shadows, comic-book ink technique"*; ban "soft painterly blending, airbrushed."

## 11. Ink-and-atmosphere 2D (Hollow Knight register)
**Made:** hand-drawn clean linework in deep multi-layer parallax scenes, unified in-engine by dynamic light, fog, particles, desaturated grade — flat drawings made volumetric by atmosphere.
**Looks:** simple elegant line-drawn figures, cute-melancholy, big empty eyes; monochrome blue-grey-black + one intense glowing accent (pale soul-white, infection-orange); dark foreground silhouettes, misty receding layers; drifting spores/dust/motes; soft lantern light pools in oppressive dark; gothic-organic ruins.
**Cue:** *hand-drawn 2D game art, melancholy underground kingdom — small elegant character with clean ink linework, monochrome blue-grey palette with one glowing pale accent, layered parallax cavern depth, dark foreground silhouettes, drifting luminous particles and mist, soft lantern light pools in darkness, gothic organic ruins*
**Fail→fix:** palette blooms colorful → *"restricted monochromatic palette, desaturated blues and blacks, single glowing accent only"*; ban "colorful, warm daylight."

## 12. Cozy-game aesthetic (Animal Crossing/Stardew register)
**Made:** two paths, one register — soft-lit rounded toy-like 3D (curved-world camera, pastel ambient), or warm 16-bit pixel with seasonal palettes; directed toward softness and zero threat.
**Looks:** rounded chibi/toy proportions, no sharp corners anywhere; sage/cream/butter/terracotta pastel-earth palette; warm golden ambient, no harsh shadows; cozy props (gardens, string lights, mailboxes); seasonal color storytelling; matte gentle rendering, mild bloom.
**Cue:** *cozy life-sim game art, soft rounded toylike 3D style — chibi proportions, pastel and earthy palette of sage cream and butter yellow, warm golden ambient lighting, charming village scene with gardens and string lights, matte gentle rendering, no harsh shadows, wholesome storybook calm* (pixel variant: *warm 16-bit pixel art farm scene, seasonal palette*)
**Fail→fix:** detail creep turns it "cinematic" → *"simple matte surfaces, soft flat lighting, low contrast, toy-like simplicity"*; ban "photorealistic, dramatic lighting, gritty."

## 13. AAA cinematic realism (The Last of Us register)
**Made:** full photoreal pipeline (scanned PBR, real-time GI, volumetrics) — signature is COLOR SCRIPTING: palettes and light motivation assigned per story beat; naturalistic, film-graded, deliberately drab.
**Looks:** pore-level skin, worn fabric, wet asphalt, moss; motivated shafts through broken windows/ceilings, soft GI bounce with color bleed; dusty volumetrics, backlit rain, flashlight beams; muted greens/greys/ambers, teal shadow tint, gentle grain; nature-reclaiming-ruins set dressing; shallow DOF, OTS/medium-close framing.
**Cue:** *AAA video game cinematic still, photorealistic render — weathered survivor in overgrown ruined city, motivated light shaft through broken ceiling, volumetric dust, realistic PBR skin and fabric, muted desaturated film grade with amber-teal tones, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, somber grounded atmosphere*
**Fail→fix:** glossy CG-plastic skin, over-lit → bias to film language: *"naturalistic lighting, filmic grade, imperfect skin, matte surfaces"*; ban "glossy CGI, oversaturated, plastic skin." (For fully photoreal human briefs, go to `references/frame_realism_engine.md` instead.)

## 14. Key-art & UI framing conventions
**Made:** marketing illustration composed to sell mood + genre + protagonist at thumbnail size; layout templates reserve dead zones for logos/HUD; silhouette and value hierarchy checked at postage-stamp scale.

# Nano Banana — illustration & graphic-design deep registers

> **STATUS: UNVALIDATED (researched 2026-07-09, agent research round).** Extends `references/graphic_and_illustration_styles.md` and `stylized_render_and_animation_looks.md` §3/§3b. Cross-cutting laws: **name the reproduction technology, not just the era** (offset lithograph, copperplate engraving, aerosol stencil, chromolithograph); **palette counts are load-bearing** ("3 spot colors: X, Y, Z" prevents full-gamut drift); **one artifact clause per prompt** (misregistration, overspray, plate marks, foxing, paper grain); typography-heavy registers: quote short real words per `references/text_rendering.md` or prompt "minimal text".

## 1. Editorial illustration (New Yorker / NYT op-ed register)
One central conceptual twist — the image IS the argument; muted 3–5-color desaturated palette + one accent; generous flat negative space; wobbly hand-drawn contour, flat fills with paper-grain/dry-brush texture; deadpan wit.
**Cue:** *editorial magazine-cover illustration — conceptual visual metaphor, flat muted palette of dusty teal, ochre, brick red and cream, hand-drawn wobbly contour lines, quiet negative space, subtle paper-grain texture, gouache-flat fills, deadpan understated mood, no text, single strong idea*
**Fail→fix:** drifts to saturated startup vector, loses the idea → name the metaphor explicitly (*"a man watering a houseplant shaped like a smartphone"*), ban "corporate vector art, gradient, glossy."

## 2. Corporate Memphis / flat tech (Alegria register)
Disproportionate figures — tiny heads, huge noodle limbs, oversized hands; non-representational skin (blue, purple, coral); flat fills, zero gradients or shadows; joyful-productivity scenes; floaty abstract props.
**Cue:** *corporate Memphis flat vector illustration — characters with small heads and exaggerated bendy limbs, purple and coral skin tones, completely flat solid fills, no gradients, no outlines, pastel background, oversized abstract plants and floating shapes, cheerful SaaS landing-page aesthetic*
**Fail→fix:** anatomy normalizes + shading appears → front-load *"exaggerated disproportionate limbs, tiny head, flat 2D, no shading, no realistic anatomy."*

## 3. Mid-century modern advertising (50s–60s register)
Hand-separated limited-color offset printing — 3–5 spot colors (mustard, teal, tomato, charcoal, cream) deliberately off-register; loose calligraphic brush lines not aligned with fills; bean-head dot-eye geometric humans (Mary Blair / Jim Flora / UPA lineage); dry-brush/crayon/stipple fills; atomic starbursts; optimistic Americana.
**Cue:** *1950s mid-century modern advertising illustration — limited palette of mustard, teal, tomato red and charcoal on cream, misregistered offset print with color shapes offset from loose black brush linework, dry-brush and crayon texture, bean-headed dot-eye characters, atomic starburst motifs, playful retro Americana*
**Fail→fix:** clean modern "retro-themed" vector → demand the print artifacts (*"visible misregistration, ink texture, hand-separated color plates"*) + name the lineage ("Mary Blair style").

## 4. Bauhaus / Swiss International Typographic
Mathematical grid, asymmetric layout, flush-left type; geometric primitives only, one dominant oversized shape; primary red/blue/yellow + B/W (Bauhaus) or restrained two-color (Swiss); extreme scale contrast; flat ink, zero texture or decoration.
**Cue:** *Swiss International Typographic Style poster — strict mathematical grid, asymmetric composition, giant geometric circle and diagonal bars, red black and white on off-white, Helvetica-style sans-serif flush-left, extreme scale contrast, flat solid color, generous negative space, 1960s Zurich concert poster*
**Fail→fix:** garbled fake letterforms (type is load-bearing) → give short real quoted words on Pro, or *"abstract geometric composition, minimal text"* + type in post.

## 5. Psychedelic 60s poster (Fillmore register)
Melting lettering filling every contour, near-illegible; vibrating equal-value complementary pairs (acid green/magenta, orange/blue); horror-vacui density; Art Nouveau whiplash curves + flowing-hair profiles; flat offset ink, obsessively contoured.
**Cue:** *1960s psychedelic concert poster — melting liquid lettering flowing to fill every space, vibrating complementary colors acid green against hot magenta at equal value, dense swirling art-nouveau curves, woman's profile with flowing hair, concentric ripples, flat offset lithograph ink, no empty space, optical vibration*
**Fail→fix:** generic tie-dye/fractal glow → anchor in print (*"flat offset lithograph, poster, hand-lettered"*), ban "3D render, glow, fractal, airbrush."

## 6. Art Deco poster (Cassandre register)
Stencil-and-airbrush lithography reducing subjects to geometric volumes; monumental low-angle heroism; sunbursts, zigzags, stepped forms; black/cream + one or two accents (cobalt, vermilion, gold); integrated geometric caps lettering.
**Cue:** *1930s Art Deco travel poster — subject viewed from dramatic low angle, radical geometric simplification into cones and cylinders, smooth airbrushed gradients on steel-gray forms, sunburst rays, stepped zigzag border, black cream cobalt and vermilion, monumental machine-age grandeur, flat lithograph finish*
**Fail→fix:** drifts to Gatsby gold-filigree party graphics → specify *"Cassandre-style lithograph poster"* + machine-age subject; ban "gold ornament, filigree."

## 7. Botanical / scientific illustration
Watercolor or hand-colored stipple engraving over precise graphite; taxonomically diagnostic — specimen isolated on cream, with dissections and stages as numbered figures; fine engraving lines under transparent washes; Latin binomial caption; plate mark, foxing.
**Cue:** *19th-century botanical illustration, hand-colored copperplate engraving with watercolor — single [plant] specimen isolated on aged cream paper, dissected seed pod and root as separate numbered figures, fine stipple shading, delicate transparent washes, Latin name in copperplate script, plate mark and subtle foxing*
**Fail→fix:** pretty loose watercolor of a flower → insist *"engraving linework under watercolor, dissection details, figure labels, plain background"*; ban "bouquet, vase, wet-on-wet."

## 8. Tattoo registers (three, never mixed)
- **American traditional:** *bold thick black outlines, solid flat fills in red yellow and green only, [motif] with banner scroll reading "[WORD]", black whip shading, aged flash-sheet paper, old-school 2D emblem.* Fail: lines thin into neo-trad → *"thick uniform outline, flat solid color, max 4 colors, no gradients."*
- **Fine-line:** *single-needle style, delicate hairline black strokes, minimal soft gray shading, small elegant [motif], abundant negative space, precise minimalist linework.* Fail: bolds into blackwork → *"ultra-thin lines, no bold outlines, no solid black fill."*
- **Irezumi:** *traditional Japanese full back-piece composition — koi ascending a waterfall among scalloped waves and swirling wind bars, cherry blossoms, bold black outlines, dense black background with smooth bokashi gradients, muted red indigo and ochre, flows with body contours.* Fail: motifs float isolated → *"waves, clouds and wind bars filling all space between motifs, unified full-coverage composition."*

## 9. Graffiti / street art registers (three)
- **Wildstyle:** *complex interlocking 3D letters spelling "[WORD]", arrows and spikes weaving through letterforms, chrome and hot-orange gradient fill with shine highlights and bubbles, heavy black 3D drop blocks, cyan halo outline, spray drips, brick wall, 1980s NYC burner.* Fail: readable bubble letters → *"extremely complex interlocking, barely legible, advanced wildstyle."*
- **Stencil (Banksy-type):** *black spray-paint stencil on weathered concrete, high-contrast posterized photographic shading, visible stencil bridges and slight overspray, single red accent object, satirical juxtaposition, raw wall texture through the paint.* Fail: clean digital vector on white → put the wall in the prompt.
- **Contemporary mural:** *massive mural covering a five-story building side, portrait blended with flat geometric shapes and patterns, photoreal face transitioning to graphic abstraction, spray texture, street and pedestrians below for scale.* Fail: scale collapses to canvas → include building, windows, street-level viewers.

## 10. Fantasy book-cover oil (Frazetta register)
Traditional oils on toned ground — central heroic figure mid-action with torqued coiled anatomy; dark earthy palette (umber, ochre, blood red) with one blazing focal zone; chiaroscuro rim light against murky swirling atmosphere; crisp brushwork at focal point, smoky suggestion everywhere else; low horizon, triangle composition.
**Cue:** *epic fantasy oil painting, 1970s paperback cover — muscular hero mid-action atop vanquished foes, torqued dynamic anatomy, dramatic chiaroscuro rim light against a murky umber and blood-red sky, loose expressive brushwork dissolving at edges, tight rendering on the focal figure, visible oil impasto, pulp sword-and-sorcery grandeur*
**Fail→fix:** slick modern digital concept art → *"traditional oils, visible brushstrokes, loose edges, painted on toned board, 1970s print grain"*; ban "digital painting, airbrush, ArtStation."

## 11. D&D rulebook art (5e register)
Polished digital fantasy painting, character-first staging with readable silhouettes; equipment legibility (every buckle inventoriable); saturated grounded color with glowing spell-light focal accents; painterly backdrop fading to vignette; heroic PG-13 register.
**Cue:** *official fantasy rulebook interior illustration — adventuring party in dynamic readable poses facing a [monster] in torchlit dungeon, clean rendered armor and equipment detail, saturated colors with glowing spell-light accents, painterly background fading to vignette, heroic tabletop RPG art*
**Fail→fix:** drifts grimdark-photoreal or cartoon, gear turns to mush → name the register (*"clean readable design"*) + enumerate gear; ban whichever drift appears.

## 12. Tarot card (Rider–Waite–Smith register)
Pen-and-ink with flat watercolor blocking, 1909 color lithography; bold uniform outlines, flattened stage-like perspective; golden-yellow skies, slate blue, red robes; lone symbolic medieval figure; encoded symbols; card frame with Roman numeral top + title caption bottom; aged cardstock.
**Cue:** *tarot card illustration in the 1909 Rider–Waite–Smith style — "[THE MOON]", bold black ink outlines with flat watercolor color blocking, flattened medieval theatrical perspective, golden-yellow sky, lone symbolic figure with esoteric symbols, rectangular border with Roman numeral at top and title caption at bottom, aged lithograph cardstock*
**Fail→fix:** ornate modern gilt fantasy tarot → pin date + technique (*"1909 lithograph, flat unshaded color, simple black outline"*); ban "gold foil, filigree, glow."

## 13. Vintage matchbox / label art
Miniature chromolithography, ≤4 flat spot inks on cheap absorbent paper; one bold central emblem in a decorative border; chunky hand-lettered multilingual brand type; corner flourishes, price roundels; ink bleed, scuffing, misregistration; regional flavors (Meiji Japanese, Soviet constructivist, Indian deity-label).
**Cue:** *vintage matchbox label, mid-century chromolithograph — bold [tiger] emblem inside ornamental rectangular border, flat spot colors vermilion red black and buff paper with slight misregistration, chunky hand-lettered "SAFETY MATCHES" text, corner flourishes, coarse cheap paper with ink bleed and scuffed edges, 1950s [Japanese/Soviet/Indian] printing*
**Fail→fix:** crisp modern vintage-style badge → enforce production constraints (*"maximum 3 ink colors, misregistered, ink bleed, tiny label format"*), pick ONE regional tradition.

**Fail→fix:** muddy brown sludge, no focal point → *"one strong glowing accent element, clear rim light separating character from background."*

# Nano Banana — NPR & animation-production technique registers

> **STATUS: UNVALIDATED (researched 2026-07-09, agent research round).** Validate per the critique rubric in `references/composition_posing_and_critique.md`.
> Universal negative for every register here: *no photorealistic PBR shading, no glossy specular, no realistic depth-of-field* — gloss and lens blur drag every NPR prompt back to default-render look. One register per image.

## 1. Toon / cel shading (3D)
**Made:** lighting ramp quantized — smooth falloff remapped to 2–4 stepped bands; paired with outline pass, flat specular.
**Looks:** hard terminator between lit/shadow zones; 2–3 flat tones per surface; shadow shapes read as graphic cuts; single crisp banded specular; saturated (color-shifted, not just darker) shadows; 3D volume under flat paint.
**Cue:** *cel-shaded 3D render, toon shader — two-tone stepped lighting, hard shadow terminator, flat color bands, graphic cut shadow shapes, crisp banded specular, saturated colored shadows, zero gradient falloff*
**Fail→fix:** drifts to smooth PBR → *"posterized lighting, exactly two shadow tones, no soft gradients."*

## 2. Outline / ink shader
**Made:** contour lines via inverted-hull or edge detection on depth/normal buffers; constant or tapering weight.
**Looks:** continuous dark contour on every silhouette; interior lines only on strong creases; colored line (navy/brown) beats pure black; drawn-over-3D read.
**Cue:** *3D render with ink outline shader — bold contour lines on every silhouette, tapered interior crease lines, uniform line weight, dark navy ink lines, no photoreal edge softness*
**Fail→fix:** lines only on character, not world → *"ink outlines on all objects and background, unified line weight across the frame."*

## 3. Matcap
**Made:** shading looked up from a pre-lit sphere image by screen-space normals — camera-locked, identical on every object.
**Looks:** one hyper-consistent studio material (chrome / ZBrush red wax / pearl); baked reflections matching no scene; no cast shadows; sculpt-viewport maquette feel; void background.
**Cue:** *matcap render, sculpt viewport look — single uniform red-wax matcap material, baked sphere lighting, no cast shadows, floating digital maquette on flat void*
**Fail→fix:** scene lighting/shadows creep in → *"screen-space sphere lighting only, object casts no shadow, 3D sculpting software screenshot."*

## 4. Clay + AO render
**Made:** all materials overridden to matte gray clay, soft light, heavy ambient occlusion — the look-dev blocking pass.
**Looks:** uniform matte putty-gray; AO darkening every crevice/contact; no color/texture/specular; pure sculptural form.
**Cue:** *clay render with ambient occlusion — uniform matte gray material override, soft AO in crevices and contact points, no textures, no specular, soft overcast studio light, pure sculptural form*
**Fail→fix:** eyes/face render realistic while body stays clay → *"entire figure including eyes and hair is unpainted matte clay, blank sculptural eyes."*

## 5. Wireframe render
**Made:** mesh edges rendered as visible lines — alone on black, or overlaid on clay (hidden-line).
**Looks:** quad grid flowing along the form; edge loops around eyes/mouth/joints; denser mesh at detail; glowing-on-void or thin-dark-on-clay variants.
**Cue:** *wireframe render — visible quad topology, clean edge loops following anatomy, thin wireframe lines over matte clay base, 3D viewport hidden-line style*
**Fail→fix:** random crosshatch instead of topology → *"structured quad mesh, edge loops following surface curvature, Blender viewport wireframe overlay."*

## 6. Mixed-media (2D FX over 3D, boiling lines, on-twos energy)
**Made:** CG base + hand-drawn frame-by-frame FX composited on top; linework redrawn per frame so it "boils"; motion held on twos (Arcane, Mitchells vs the Machines).
**Looks:** 3D characters wearing wobbly hand-drawn contours; FX as flat 2–3-tone graphic shapes with drawn edges; scribbled emphasis marks, action lines, drawn glints; painterly surface texture.
**Cue:** *mixed-media hybrid animation still — 3D character with hand-drawn 2D overlay, boiling sketchy ink lines, flat two-tone hand-drawn smoke and FX shapes, scribbled highlights and action lines, animated-on-twos energy*
**Fail→fix:** FX render as photoreal volumetric smoke → *"effects are flat graphic hand-drawn shapes with hard edges, cel-style two-tone smoke, no volumetric simulation."*
*(In stills, on-twos/boil translate to: smear frames, multiple limbs, speed lines, jittered linework.)*

## 7. Cutout / paper animation (South Park / Gilliam)
**Made:** flat cut shapes pivoting at joints like paper puppets; originally construction paper / collaged photo scraps stop-motioned.
**Looks:** construction-paper texture with fibrous cut edges; bodies from simple geometric pieces with visible overlaps and drop shadows between layers; flat primary colors; frontal staging; photo-collage in the Gilliam variant.
**Cue:** *paper cutout animation style — flat construction-paper shapes with rough scissor-cut edges, visible paper fiber, layered pieces with tiny drop shadows between them, simple geometric bodies, flat primary colors*
**Fail→fix:** renders as smooth flat vector → *"real cardstock texture, hard cut edges, shadow gaps where paper layers overlap, stop-motion collage photograph."*

## 8. Rotoscope look (A Scanner Darkly / Waking Life)
**Made:** live-action traced/painted frame-by-frame — real motion, proportions, and lens perspective survive under a drawn skin; shading simplified into flat vector patches.
**Looks:** photographically accurate proportions and perspective under hand-drawn surface; faces broken into posterized color patches with drifting boundaries; thick unstable contours; shadow zones that float; uncanny real-but-drawn tension; muted realistic palette.
**Cue:** *rotoscoped live-action look — traced-over-photograph accuracy, flat posterized color patches on the face, thick drifting vector outlines, floating shadow shapes, muted realistic palette*
**Fail→fix:** becomes generic cartoon portrait → *"photorealistic proportions and camera perspective preserved, only the surface is painted — realistic lens geometry, drawn skin."*

## 9. Motion-graphics / flat animation
**Made:** vector shapes (AE/Illustrator pipeline) — geometric characters, flat fills, limited palettes; explainer-video lineage.
**Looks:** flat fills, zero or single-weight strokes; circle heads, capsule limbs, oversized hands; 4–6-color brand palette; optional grain on flat fills; generous negative space; clean bezier curves.
**Cue:** *flat vector motion-graphics style — clean geometric shapes, flat color fills, limited 5-color palette, capsule-limbed characters, subtle grain on flat fills, generous negative space, no outlines, no gradients*
**Fail→fix:** 3D shading creeps in → *"completely flat, no shading, no gradients, no 3D depth, poster-flat vector shapes only."*

## 10. Comic-print hybrid 3D (Spider-Verse lineage)
**Made:** CG art-directed like a printed comic — hand-inked lines + hatching on 3D, Ben-Day halftone replaces soft gradients, misregistered CMYK edges mimic offset printing, no motion blur (smears/multiples instead).
**Looks:** halftone dots inside shadows/blur/skin; hand-inked hatching on shading zones; red-blue misregistration fringing instead of DOF blur; graphic sky shapes; hyper-saturated comic palette; printed texture over dimensional form.
**Cue:** *comic-print 3D render — Ben-Day halftone dots in the shadows, hand-inked hatching over CG forms, CMYK misregistration fringing instead of depth-of-field blur, no motion blur, saturated comic-book palette, screen-printed 3D frame*
**Fail→fix:** halftone applied as uniform screen-door over everything → *"halftone only inside shadow areas and out-of-focus zones, dot size varies with tone, lit areas stay clean."*

## 11. Painterly-sketchy hybrid 3D (Puss in Boots: The Last Wish register)
**Made:** CG rendered as moving illustration — painted brushwork in textures and light, stepped painterly lighting, action on twos with smears and drawn FX flashes; "storybook come alive."
**Looks:** brushstroke texture in fur/skies/foliage; rim light painted as discrete strokes; dry-brush edges, gouache-flat passages; graphic 2D poses at action peaks; storybook color scripting; illustrated depth instead of lens DOF.
**Cue:** *stylized painterly hybrid-3D render — visible brushstrokes in fur and sky, painted rim-light strokes, gouache texture, dry-brush edges, storybook color palette, illustrated depth haze, hand-drawn FX flashes, zero gloss, zero photoreal DOF*
**Fail→fix:** collapses into oil-paint-filter over glossy render → kill gloss first: *"matte surfaces, no specular sheen, brushstrokes follow form direction, texture density varies (loose background, tight face)."*

## 12. Sketch-doodle concept-art 3D (TMNT: Mutant Mayhem register)
**Made:** CG deliberately made to look like a teenager's notebook drawing — misshapen linework, scribbled/marker-hatched textures on 3D, "draw like your 15-year-old self."
**Looks:** wobbly overlapping sketch lines with stray construction marks; scribble/marker/spray fills; asymmetric misshapen proportions; crosshatched shadows; grimy palette with neon pops; nothing straight or clean.
**Cue:** *sketchy concept-art 3D render — wobbly imperfect ink lines with stray construction marks, scribbled marker shading, crosshatched shadows, misshapen hand-drawn proportions, ballpoint-notebook-doodle texture over 3D forms, deliberately unpolished*
**Fail→fix:** model "corrects" into clean lines → *"lines drawn fast and crooked, redrawn overlapping strokes, teenage sketchbook rawness, do not clean up the linework."*

# Nano Banana — commercial product-render CGI registers

> **STATUS: UNVALIDATED (researched 2026-07-09, agent research round).** Validate per the critique rubric. Pairs with `references/commercial_framing_and_set_design.md` (framing, look blocks, practical elements) — this file adds the *CGI-render* registers: the deliberately-CG advertising looks where perfect geometry and simulation ARE the style.

## 1. Studio product lighting (KeyShot/Octane/V-Ray register)
**Made:** virtual infinity cove + HDRI + draggable area lights; artists sculpt exactly where highlights land; classic 3-point (large soft key 45°, dim fill, rim behind).
**Looks:** long soft rectangular softbox reflections on gloss; gradient sweep raking a curved edge; seamless backdrop, soft contact shadow + subtle floor reflection; dark-premium variant with thin rim highlights tracing edges; zero noise, correct Fresnel; slight vignette.
**Cue:** *premium studio product render — seamless dark charcoal infinity backdrop, three-point softbox lighting, long rectangular softbox reflections sweeping across glossy surfaces, crisp rim light tracing the silhouette, gradient light sweep on curved edges, soft contact shadow, subtle floor reflection, advertising grade*
**Fail→fix:** silhouette melts into dark background → *"bright rim light separating every edge, thin white edge highlights on top and side contours."*

## 2. Exploded-view technical render
**Made:** assembly components translated apart along one axis, alignment kept; leader lines optional.
**Looks:** parts hovering in strict axial alignment, evenly spaced; internals visible (PCBs, screws, gaskets, coils); neutral studio ground; each part casts its own soft shadow; machine detail (knurling, threads, pins).
**Cue:** *exploded view technical render of [product], components separated and floating in precise axial alignment, evenly spaced, internal PCB, screws, seals and housing visible, thin dotted assembly-axis lines, clean studio lighting, dark gradient background, CAD-quality detail*
**Fail→fix:** invented nonsense internals → enumerate the real stack in order (*"top to bottom: lens, retaining ring, sensor PCB, battery cell, aluminum housing"*).

## 3. X-ray / cutaway render
**Made:** shell made semi-transparent (ghost shader) or boolean-cut with a clipping plane; internals render opaque.
**Looks:** translucent frosted shell with opaque internals; cross-section plane with highlighted cut edge; internals fully lit; technical color coding; dark blueprint-adjacent mood with cyan accents.
**Cue:** *cutaway x-ray render — outer shell as translucent frosted ghost material, internal components opaque and brightly lit, cross-section with glowing cut edge, color-coded parts, dark background with cyan accent lighting, engineering visualization*
**Fail→fix:** everything turns muddy overlapping glass → *"ONLY the outer housing is transparent; all internal parts solid, opaque, brightly lit"* — one shell layer max.

## 4. Liquid & splash simulation (beverage register)
**Made:** FLIP/SPH fluid sims frozen at the hero frame; refractive shaders; droplets art-directed by hand.
**Looks:** symmetrical crown splash with beaded rim tips; helical pour wrapping the bottle; suspended droplet fields refracting the background; thin sheets with surface-tension-ragged edges; backlit translucency; condensation on the container; ingredients caught mid-air.
**Cue:** *high-speed CGI liquid simulation render — dynamic [liquid] splash frozen mid-motion wrapping around [product], symmetrical crown splash at the base, hundreds of suspended refractive droplets, thin liquid sheets with surface-tension edges, condensation on the bottle, backlit translucent liquid, photoreal caustics, advertising key visual*
**Fail→fix:** liquid reads as solid gel ribbon → *"breakup into fine spray and individual droplets at the edges, varied droplet sizes, transparent refractive liquid, thin water-like viscosity."*

## 5. Cosmetics render language (smears, goop, texture pulls)
**Made:** cream/gloss/balm sculpted or simmed as viscous meshes; SSS + thin-film/pearlescent shaders; macro lens.
**Looks:** sculptural cream swipe with lifted trailing peak; glossy goop drips draping the jar; pearlescent micro-glitter at macro scale; subsurface glow in creams; monochrome color-blocked set matching the shade; serum bubbles suspended in a clear blob.
**Cue:** *luxury cosmetics CGI render, macro texture pull with sculptural swipe and lifted peak, viscous glossy goop draping the jar, pearlescent pigment shimmer with visible micro-glitter flecks, subsurface scattering in the cream, suspended micro-bubbles, monochromatic set matching product shade, soft beauty lighting*
**Fail→fix:** cream reads as opaque plastic frosting → *"soft subsurface scattering, slightly translucent at thin edges, satin sheen not hard gloss, whipped and holds soft peaks."*

## 6. Jewelry rendering (caustics + macro sparkle)
**Made:** path tracing with high refraction bounces, correct IOR (diamond 2.42) and dispersion enabled; small crisp area lights create defined caustics; macro DOF.
**Looks:** rainbow spectral fire inside facets; sharp caustic patterns cast beneath the stone; star-point sparkles on facet edges; mirror metal with elongated softbox reflections; one facet plane sharp, prongs in bokeh; dark velvet ground.
**Cue:** *ultra-macro jewelry CGI render — physically accurate gemstone refraction with spectral dispersion and rainbow fire inside facets, crisp caustic light patterns cast below, star sparkle on facet edges, mirror-polished metal with soft studio reflections, shallow macro depth of field, dark reflective surface*
**Fail→fix:** diamonds render as grey glass → *"internal rainbow dispersion, bright white facet flashes, high contrast between dark and brilliant facets"* — contrast reads as brilliance.

## 7. Automotive CGI
**Made:** lit almost entirely indirectly — huge overhead softboxes/panels reflected in paint; light streaks painted onto the HDRI so one continuous highlight runs the body line.
**Looks:** one long unbroken light streak hugging the body line; clearcoat = sharp mirror layer over metallic flake; dark glossy floor reflection; environment exists only in reflections; crisp horizon line across the doors; rim-lit wheels, dark arches.
**Cue:** *automotive CGI studio render — [car] on dark reflective studio floor, single continuous light streak sweeping the entire body line, giant overhead softbox reflection, glossy clearcoat with metallic flake, crisp horizon reflection across the doors, indirect lighting only, dramatic dark background*
**Fail→fix:** blotchy reflections chop the body line → *"one smooth continuous specular highlight following the shoulder line, clean uncluttered reflections."*

## 8. Food CGI
**Made:** modeled/scanned food with heavy SSS; Houdini dynamics for cheese pulls, drips, levitating ingredient explosions, steam volumes; strong soft key + rim.
**Looks:** deconstructed ingredient explosion in ordered mid-air stack; stretchy tapering cheese pull; glossy sauce frozen mid-drip; backlit volumetric steam; SSS glow in tomato/citrus/frosting; suspended crumbs and micro-splashes.
**Cue:** *photorealistic food CGI render — deconstructed ingredients levitating in a perfect mid-air stack, glossy sauce frozen mid-drip, stretching cheese pull with tapering strands, backlit volumetric steam, subsurface scattering in tomato and frosting, suspended crumbs, strong soft key with rim light, dark moody food-ad background*
**Fail→fix:** waxy fake food → *"natural surface imperfections — irregular char, uneven crumb, oil micro-droplets, matte-to-gloss variation"* (uniform gloss is the #1 CGI-food tell).

## 9. Soft-body / inflatable 3D typography
**Made:** text meshes inflated via cloth-sim pressure — pillow forms with natural seams/wrinkles; glossy PVC, chrome, or matte rubber shaders.
**Looks:** puffy pillow letters with tight inflation wrinkles at corners/seams; glossy vinyl or chrome balloon material with big soft reflections; letters squishing together; candy/pastel or monochrome sets; soft dome light.
**Cue:** *inflatable 3D typography render, the word "[WORD]" as puffy balloon letters, soft-body inflation with tight wrinkles at seams and corners, glossy PVC vinyl with large soft studio reflections, letters gently squishing together, pastel monochrome background, soft dome lighting* (quote the text + name the font per `references/text_rendering.md`)
**Fail→fix:** rigid extruded 3D text with shiny material → stress deformation: *"over-inflated, bulging, pillow-quilted surfaces, no flat faces, no hard edges anywhere."*

## 10. Satisfying-render aesthetic
**Made:** primitive geometry + rigid/soft-body sims with oddly-precise fits; clean studio light, pastel palettes; ASMR-adjacent loop culture (C4D + Redshift lineage).
**Looks:** perfect primitives in exact grids; objects nesting into matching cavities with zero clearance; matte clay/satin materials; 2–3-color pastel palette; isometric or dead-center symmetry; soft AO contact shadows; implied mid-slice/mid-squish motion.
**Cue:** *oddly satisfying 3D render — perfect geometric primitives in an exact grid, objects nesting precisely into matching cavities, soft matte clay materials, pastel two-tone palette, dead-center symmetrical camera, soft studio light with gentle AO shadows, seamless-loop aesthetic, flawless geometry*
**Fail→fix:** busy asymmetric composition kills the effect → hard-constrain: *"strict symmetry, maximum 3 colors, maximum 2 object types, generous negative space."*

# Nano Banana — physically-lit feature CG register (simulated-GI animation look)

> **STATUS: UNVALIDATED (drafted 2026-07-09, research pass only).** No field round scored yet. Validate with the critique rubric in `references/composition_posing_and_critique.md`, then update or strike.
>
> **Reference films for how this look is meant** (inspiration pointers, NOT the register name — don't put film titles in prompts): the Disney-Hyperion era of feature CG (e.g. *Big Hero 6*) is the canonical example of this lighting philosophy.
>
> **Never mix with the Arcane / Fortiche register (`style_arcane_fortiche.md`).** Opposite philosophies: here light is fully SIMULATED by a path-tracing renderer; there it is hand-PAINTED onto textures. One register per image.

## How it's made (prompt-relevant mechanics)

- **Light is fully simulated.** Path-tracing renderers (Disney's Hyperion is the archetype) compute global illumination with massive multi-bounce light transport. Every glow in the frame is a physically-plausible source bouncing — nothing is painted on. Describe sources and bounce, and the register follows.
- **Energy-conserving subsurface scattering.** Light visibly transmits through translucent materials — through an ear, through translucent vinyl, through paper lanterns and frosted plastic. Translucency-under-light is a signature cue of this rendering class.
- **Dense-city night = thousands of REAL lights.** The archetype film lit ~83,000 procedural buildings with 216,000+ individual street lights — the night-city look is dense fields of small practical sources (windows, neon, lanterns, streetlights), each casting true soft illumination with its own falloff, not one painted glow wash. Prompt night scenes as *many small sources, each with its own soft pool*.
- **Stylized characters, believable light.** Character design stays rounded, simple, appealing (never photoreal humans); the *light and materials* behave physically. That contrast IS the look.
- **Palette discipline.** Character-matched color palettes; overall grade warm, saturated-but-natural, optimistic.

## How it looks

Warm, soft, optimistic, photoreal-adjacent CG. Believable bounced daylight on rounded appealing stylized characters. Golden sunsets over detailed environments; night scenes glowing with dense multicolored practicals casting real soft light onto faces and wet streets; translucent materials that visibly transmit light; clean smooth surfaces, physically-plausible soft shadows, polished finish — never gritty, never brushy.

**Lighting recipes:**

| Scene | Light recipe |
|---|---|
| Day exterior | soft multi-bounce daylight, atmospheric haze, warm sun key + cool sky fill, gentle contact shadows |
| Golden hour | low warm key across the environment, long soft shadows, glowing window reflections |
| Night street | hundreds of visible practicals — neon signage, lanterns, lit windows, streetlights — each with its own soft pool and falloff, mixed color temperatures, faces lit by the nearest source |
| Translucency beat | strong source behind a translucent material (vinyl, lantern paper, frosted plastic) with visible subsurface glow-through |
| Interior emotional beat | single warm practical (lamp/screen) as key, simulated bounce filling the room softly, cool ambient spill from a window |

## Cue block (paste-ready)

> *polished feature-CG render with fully simulated global illumination — soft multi-bounce light, warm optimistic grade, subsurface scattering through translucent vinyl and paper materials; rounded appealing stylized characters; environment dense with practical light sources at night, each casting real soft glow with its own falloff; clean smooth surfaces, physically-plausible soft shadows*

## Prompt skeleton

> *"A [subject] in polished feature-CG animation style — rounded appealing stylized design, soft skin with subsurface scattering, lit by simulated global illumination: [golden-hour bounce / a night street dense with neon signs, lanterns, and lit windows, each source casting its own soft pool of light]. [Scene/action] in [environment]. Warm optimistic grade, clean smooth surfaces, physically-plausible soft shadows. No brushstroke texture, no gritty painterly finish, no photographic grain."*

## Register police

If output comes back textured, brushy, theatrical-dark, or concept-art-like, it has slid toward the painterly/Arcane register. Fix: restate *clean smooth surfaces, simulated soft bounce light, warm optimistic grade, physically-plausible shadows* and **ban**: *painterly, brushstroke, gritty, matte-painted, concept-art, theatrical*.

Don't render studio-owned characters; cast original characters in the register. Film titles stay out of the prompt — the descriptive block carries the look.

# Nano Banana — Arcane / Fortiche register (painted-texture hybrid 3D)

> **STATUS: UNVALIDATED (drafted 2026-07-09, research pass only).** No field round scored yet. Validate with the critique rubric in `references/composition_posing_and_critique.md`, then update or strike.
>
> **Never mix with the Big Hero 6 / Hyperion register (`style_big_hero_6_hyperion.md`).** They are opposite philosophies: Arcane's light is PAINTED onto textures by hand; Hyperion's light is SIMULATED by a physical renderer. One register per image.

## How it's made (prompt-relevant mechanics)

- **Hand-painted textures carry the light.** 3D character models and sets are finished by classically-trained painters who paint highlights and shadows *directly onto the texture maps* — the renderer does not generate the lighting look. Light is authored like an oil painting, not simulated. This is THE defining fact of the register: describe painted light, not physical light.
- **Backgrounds are matte paintings.** Concept-art-quality 2D paintings projection-mapped onto simple geometry, giving subtle parallax. Backgrounds should read as finished concept art, not as a rendered 3D environment.
- **FX are hand-drawn 2D.** Smoke, explosions, magic, chaotic scribble energy — drawn elements composited over the 3D, traditionally animated on twos (12 fps) against 24 fps characters, so effects carry a stepped, graphic energy. In a still: FX read as drawn strokes, not simulated volumetrics.
- **Live-action camera language.** Handheld shake, naturalistic zooms, grounded moves; framing follows live-action drama grammar, not cartoon staging.
- **Pipeline texture words that help:** brushstroke detail baked into skin/hair/fabric, painted rim-light strokes, painted specular (a stroke of light, not a physical highlight), chalk/crayon texture for psychological beats, watercolor-like environmental effects.

## How it looks

Oil-painting-in-motion. Visible brushstrokes in faces, clothing, walls. Dramatic theatrical lighting with painted highlight shapes — hard rim-light strokes, neon reflected as painted color, deep expressive shadow shapes with hard painted edges. Grit, texture, wear and imperfection everywhere. Gloss, smoothness, and clean-CG softness are absent by design. Adult, moody, high-contrast drama.

**Two lighting worlds (pick one per frame):**

| World | Light recipe |
|---|---|
| Piltover (topside) | warm gilded daylight, art-deco gold/cream/brass palette, tall shafts of sun through grand architecture, clean-but-painted highlights |
| Zaun (undercity) | toxic green-and-teal industrial haze, neon signage glow, chem-punk light rising from below, smog diffusion, rust and grime palette, faces half-lit by colored practicals |

## Cue block (paste-ready)

> *stylized painterly hybrid-3D render — 3D character forms with visibly hand-painted textures, brushstroke detail baked into skin, hair, and fabric; painted highlights and rim-light strokes instead of clean CG specular; moody concept-art matte-painting background; dramatic theatrical single-source key with deep painted shadow shapes; hand-drawn 2D smoke/FX elements; gritty texture, zero gloss, zero photoreal skin*

## Prompt skeleton

> *"A [subject] in painterly hybrid-3D animation style — 3D forms finished with hand-painted textures, visible brushstrokes in the face and clothing, painted rim-light strokes along [side]. [Scene/action] against a moody matte-painted background of [location]. Theatrical [warm-gold gilded daylight / toxic teal-green neon undercity] key from [direction], deep painted shadow shapes with hard edges, hand-drawn wisps of smoke in the foreground. Gritty concept-art finish — no clean CG gloss, no photorealism, no soft feature-animation rendering."*

## Register police

If output comes back smooth, glossy, rounded, cute, or soft-lit, it has slid into the Pixar-soft/feature-CG register. Fix: restate *visible brushstrokes, painted light, gritty texture, theatrical shadow shapes* and **ban**: *smooth, polished, glossy, cute, rounded, soft global illumination, subsurface scattering*.

Do not render League of Legends / Arcane characters — cast original characters in the register. Use the descriptive block; the show name is optional reinforcement only.

# Nano Banana — Tonal families (the editorial tone atlas)

Tone is the *color, light, and mood* register of a frame — independent of subject. Two photos of the same person can read as old-money mocha or fluorescent-green institutional depending on tone alone. This file is the atlas: 16 named families, the five dials that define any of them, which tones serve which subjects, how to read tone off a reference without stealing its scene — and the two field-validated doctrines that make a frame read as a *real photograph* rather than a render.

> **Field-validated (Round 14, `field_findings.md`) — ImagineArt, 2026-07-06.** A named family — "old-money mocha" (warm mocha-cream palette, low saturation, tobacco shadows, no vivid anchor) — scored PASS (strong, 21/22) on an editorial portrait; the tone held independent of subject.

**One tonal family per image.** Stacking two grades muddies both. Pick a family, paste its phrase, then spend your remaining specificity on subject and light.

Cross-references, never duplicated here: lens character, film-grain library, diffusion filters, and the extended grade board → `references/optics_grain_and_light.md`; named film looks (Blade Runner 2049, Moonlight, Se7en…) and colorist techniques → `references/film_look_references.md`; the four creative-director levers → `references/creative_director_controls.md`; realism stack and UGC → `references/realism_and_ugc.md`.

---

## The five-variable tone model

Every tonal family is a setting of five dials. Name all five and the model has nowhere to default. Name only the family name and it averages.

| Dial | What it controls | Range to specify |
|---|---|---|
| **Hue bias** | the cast the whole frame leans toward | warm/amber · cool/teal · green · magenta · neutral · split (warm highlights, cool shadows) |
| **Skin rendering** | how flesh reads under the grade | golden · ruddy · porcelain-cool · olive · ashen · sun-flushed |
| **Contrast** | the black-to-white spread | crushed-and-punchy · low/milky · normal · high-key · chiaroscuro |
| **Saturation** | color intensity | muted/desaturated · natural · rich · hyper-saturated · monochrome |
| **Texture** | the surface of the image itself | clean digital · fine grain · heavy grain · halation · bleach-bypass crunch · soft diffusion |

**Use it as a checklist.** *"Warm amber hue bias, golden ruddy skin, crushed punchy contrast, muted saturation, fine grain"* is a complete tonal instruction before you've named a single object.

---

## The 16 named families

Each block: palette + light + mood + a paste-ready phrase. Drop the phrase in verbatim; tune the five dials from there.

**1 — Teal-noir night.** Cyan-and-black palette, warm sodium accents fighting the cyan. One practical doing the work, everything else in contaminated shadow. Mood: tense, urban, after-hours. — *"teal-noir night: cyan shadows, a single warm sodium practical, wet asphalt throwing broken reflections, blacks crushed, everything outside the light contaminated and murky."*

**2 — Sodium amber.** Monochromatic orange-brown wash, the color of old street lamps. Skin goes mask-like and warm. Mood: lonely, nocturnal, municipal. — *"sodium-vapour amber: the whole frame one orange cast, skin warmed to a mask, deep amber-brown shadows, the light pooling and falling off fast into black."*

**3 — Blue-hour flash.** Deep cobalt ambient sky against a hard cold on-camera flash freezing the subject. Mood: candid, party-edge, fashion-snapshot. — *"blue-hour flash: cobalt dusk ambient, a hard direct flash freezing the subject white against it, hot foreground falloff, cool background, snapshot energy."*

**4 — Hard amber chiaroscuro.** Single warm hard key carving the subject out of near-black. Renaissance light on a modern face. Mood: intimate, weighty, painterly. — *"hard amber chiaroscuro: one warm hard key from the side, the unlit half falling to near-black, no fill, golden highlight rolling into deep shadow."*

**5 — Terracotta earth.** Warm clay, rust, ochre, and sage — sun-baked Mediterranean/Southwest. Skin sun-flushed. Mood: grounded, organic, slow. — *"terracotta earth: clay, rust, ochre and dry sage palette, warm sun-flushed skin, dusty matte texture, natural mid-contrast."*

**6 — Cold chrome minimal.** Near-neutral with a cool steel lean, blown whites, surgical cleanliness. Skin porcelain-cool. Mood: clinical, luxury-tech, austere. — *"cold chrome minimal: neutral-cool grade, steel and white palette, porcelain skin, high-key with clean blown highlights, no grain, clinical."*

**7 — Scandinavian silver.** Soft overcast daylight, desaturated to near-grey, low contrast, blue-grey shadows. Mood: calm, hushed, design-magazine. — *"Scandinavian silver: flat overcast daylight, desaturated to near-grey, milky low contrast, cool blue-grey shadows, soft and quiet."*

**8 — Old-money mocha.** Warm browns, camel, oxblood, cream — wood, leather, brass. Skin warm and even. Mood: heritage, expensive, understated. — *"old-money mocha: camel, oxblood, cream and walnut palette, warm even skin, gentle warm contrast, soft film grain, quiet luxury."*

**9 — Pastel dawn.** Pale pinks, lilacs, and butter-yellows of early morning, low saturation, soft light, lifted blacks. Mood: tender, dreamy, hopeful. — *"pastel dawn: pale pink and lilac sky, butter-yellow low sun, lifted milky blacks, soft diffusion, gentle and hopeful."*

**10 — Monochrome-plus-one.** Strict grayscale with exactly one saturated color surviving. Mood: graphic, deliberate, editorial. Field-confirmed: close with the exclusion clause or stray color leaks in. — *"monochrome-plus-one: strictly grayscale except [the red coat], no other color anywhere — no skin tones, no warm cast — only [the red] saturated."*

**11 — Bleach-bypass concrete.** Silver-retention crunch: desaturated, high-contrast, metallic shadows, gritty. Skin ashen. Mood: brutal, war-film, institutional. — *"bleach-bypass concrete: silver-retention look, desaturated and high-contrast, metallic grey shadows, ashen skin, gritty crunch."*

**12 — Candlelit gold.** Warm flickering point source, deep falloff, amber highlights and brown shadow, a little soot in the air. Mood: period, intimate, sacred. — *"candlelit gold: a single warm flame source, amber highlights falling fast to brown shadow, soft soot haze, period-intimate, no fill light."*

**13 — Fluorescent-green institutional.** Cold green-cyan tube light, even and shadowless, slightly sickly. Skin ashen-green. Mood: bureaucratic, liminal, dread. — *"fluorescent-green institutional: overhead green-cyan tube light, flat and shadowless, sickly green cast on the skin, drop-ceiling banality."*

**14 — Overcast oyster.** Soft grey-white diffused light, pearl and putty tones, gentle low contrast, no hard shadow. Mood: editorial-calm, neutral, coastal. — *"overcast oyster: soft pearl-grey diffused daylight, putty and oyster palette, low contrast, no hard shadows, even and refined."*

**15 — Neon-rain cyberpunk.** Saturated magenta-and-cyan signage bleeding into wet streets, black sky, hard speculars. Mood: kinetic, future-noir, dense. — *"neon-rain cyberpunk: saturated magenta and cyan signage reflected in wet black streets, hard colored speculars, rain in the light, deep blacks."*

**16 — Sun-faded Kodachrome.** Warm vintage saturation gone slightly chalky, faded cyan shadows, red-yellow lean, a print left in the sun. Mood: nostalgic, Americana, memory. — *"sun-faded Kodachrome: warm red-yellow vintage saturation gone chalky, faded cyan shadows, slight print fade, 1970s memory texture."*

## Tone × subject pairing logic

Tone has to serve the subject or it fights it. The mismatches are predictable.

| Subject | Serves it | Fights it (avoid) |
|---|---|---|
| Luxury / heritage product | old-money mocha, cold chrome minimal, overcast oyster | neon-rain, fluorescent-green |
| Beauty / clean skin hero | overcast oyster, Scandinavian silver, pastel dawn | bleach-bypass, sodium amber (mask-skin) |
| Street / documentary | teal-noir, sodium amber, fluorescent-green, sun-faded Kodachrome | candlelit gold, pastel dawn |
| Period / intimate drama | candlelit gold, hard amber chiaroscuro | cold chrome, neon-rain |
| Tech / SaaS / automotive | cold chrome minimal, teal-noir, neon-rain | terracotta earth, candlelit gold |
| Food / craft / organic | terracotta earth, candlelit gold, old-money mocha | fluorescent-green, cold chrome |
| Fashion editorial | any — but commit hard; half a grade reads as a mistake | a timid, un-named "natural" grade |

**The rule:** warm intimate tones flatter people and craft; cold clean tones flatter objects and tech; contaminated/institutional tones serve realism and unease. Pick against the brief's *emotion*, not its literal content.

## Reading tone off a reference — without copying the scene

The model will happily clone a reference's subject and composition. You almost never want that. **Extract the five dials only.**

**The discipline:**
1. Look at the reference and name its five dials out loud — hue bias, skin, contrast, saturation, texture. Write *those words* into your prompt.
2. Describe **your** subject and **your** composition fresh.
3. Never write *"like the attached image"* as a whole — that copies scene, pose, and framing. Write *"using only the color and light register of the attached reference — its [warm hue bias, crushed contrast, fine grain] — applied to [my different subject]."*
4. **One tonal family per image.** If two references pull different directions, choose one for tone and use the other for subject/wardrobe only.

**Worked extraction:** reference is a moody bar portrait. Don't ask for a bar portrait. Ask: *"Apply the tonal register of the attached reference only — its amber-into-black hue bias, ruddy warmed skin, crushed shadows, and fine grain — to a wide shot of a woman reading at a kitchen table. Do not copy the reference's subject, pose, or composition."*

This is the single most common reference mistake: importing the whole picture when you wanted the *grade*.

## The real-frame override (field-validated — the biggest realism lever)

For **any brief that must read as a real film frame or a real photograph**, append this block. Decoded against large sets of actual cinema stills (Round 6); both doctrine test frames were the most convincingly real outputs of an entire 17-frame round — visually indistinguishable in register from the real stills studied. The core move is the opposite of every AI default.

Append verbatim, then tune:

> *"Shoot this as a real film frame, not a designed image. Let most of the frame go dark — darkness is allowed, even most of it. The subject can be small, back-turned, partially lost in shadow, not centered, not posed at the lens. One dominating practical light source does all the work; everything outside its reach falls to murky, contaminated shadow. No courtesy rim light, no flattering kicker, no fill. Composition is found, not designed — slightly off, like a real moment caught. Weather and atmosphere are visible only where light hits them. The palette is poor — two or three colors, not a designed scheme. A photograph, not a painting."*

**The eight principles (why each works):**

- **Darkness courage** — AI lifts every shadow to be "readable"; real frames let half the image die. Letting it go dark is the strongest single tell-killer.
- **Subject-allowed-to-be-lost** — make the subject small, back-turned, partially illegible. Writing it explicitly fights the model's pull to a centered, lit, lens-facing hero.
- **One dominating practical** — a single motivated source, not a balanced three-point rig. Real available light has one boss.
- **Murky contaminated shadows** — shadows carry color spill and noise, not clean black. *"Contaminated"* is the operative word.
- **No courtesy rim light** — the giveaway AI flatter. Ban it by name.
- **Found-not-designed composition** — off-balance, partial occlusion, imperfect framing. A real moment isn't art-directed.
- **Weather only against light** — rain, fog, dust, breath show *only* where a source backlights them, never as an even overlay (see Night physics below).
- **Palette poverty** — real scenes have two or three colors, not a designed five-color scheme. Restraint reads as real.

**Caution (Round 7):** *"back-turned"* sometimes flips the subject to face camera — write *"facing away, face not visible"* instead.

---

## Night physics (field-validated — read before any dark frame)

A doctrine frame failed on physics once: rain painted across the dark sky, the light pool over-spread, a painterly glow. The corrections below were re-tested across 10 night scenarios (sodium lot, gas-station island, headlight crossing, mercury court, kiosk window, blue hour, tungsten alley, moonlight field, overcast bus interior, underpass) and confirmed transformative. The model executes light physics well **when, and only when, the prompt states it explicitly.**

- **Inverse-square falloff.** Light dies fast. State where it ends: *"the pool ends a few meters from the pole, the rest fully black."* Don't let a source wash the whole frame.
- **Rain is backlit sparkle, not an overlay.** Rain is visible **only** as bright streaks inside a light cone. *"Rain visible only as backlit sparkle inside the light cone; the dark sky stays empty, no rain painted across it."*
- **Small hard hotspot, tight halation.** A bright source blows to a *small* hard hotspot with a tight red-orange halation ring — **not** a wide soft glow. *"A small hard blown hotspot with tight halation, not a wide soft glow."*
- **Broken speculars, not an even wash.** Wet surfaces throw broken, streaky reflections. *"Broken specular streaks on the wet asphalt, not an even sheen."*
- **Name the medium and ban the painting.** *"A photograph, not a painting"* + grain and shadow-noise kills the painterly tell. Include both in every realism night frame.
- **Constrain rain quantity with broad sources.** Rain over-renders under headlights and other broad sources — constrain with *"light drizzle, sparse."*
- **The most realistic night register tested:** high-ISO documentary — *"handheld available-light, ISO 6400, shadow noise, white balance drifting with the sources."* The underpass frame in this register passed as photojournalism.
- **Single-source scenes obey once stated.** Lamp, window, canopy, wedge, moon — all followed falloff the moment the prompt said so. State it; don't assume it.

**Pair both doctrines for the hardest realism briefs:** the real-frame override sets the *register* (darkness, lost subject, found composition); night physics enforces the *light behavior* (falloff, backlit rain, hard hotspot). Together they produce the convincingly-real night frame.

## Staging grammar

- **Depth blocking** — place people front/mid/back, not on one plane; let a foreground body partially occlude.
- **Lateral blocking** — move action across the frame (entrances from edges) rather than toward the lens.
- **Two-temperature scenes** — one warm source + one cool source in the same frame for instant depth and mood (window-cool vs lamp-warm).

## Film-frame anatomy — 9 cues that read "movie still, not photo"

1. **Aspect ratio** — 2.39:1 (or 1.85:1); render 16:9 + letterbox or crop.
2. **Motivated light** — every source visible or implied in-scene.
3. **Depth in three planes** — foreground occlusion, subject, deep background.
4. **Atmosphere** — faint haze/smoke so light has volume.
5. **Colour discipline** — one grade, 2–3 hues max.
6. **Blocking** — figures placed for the frame, not centered by default.
7. **Look-room / headroom** — cinematic negative space, not portrait-tight.
8. **Grain/halation** — a film register, not clinical digital (unless Fincher).
9. **A held moment** — mid-action or mid-thought, not posed at lens.

## The six-layer decade system (era-accurate frames)

To set a frame in a specific year, lock ALL SIX layers — one wrong layer breaks the era:

1. **Capture** — the camera/stock of the era (e.g., 1970s → grainy Kodak, slight warm cast; 1990s → consumer autofocus + flash; 2000s → early digital point-and-shoot).
2. **Grade** — the colour signature (70s amber/magenta, 80s saturated, 90s cooler, 2000s flat digital).
3. **Fashion** — period-correct wardrobe, silhouette, hair.
4. **Props** — era objects (cars, phones, packaging, signage, tech).
5. **Typography** — period fonts on any signage/labels.
6. **Body language** — posture and grooming of the time (it reads, even subtly).

**The "nothing manufactured after [year]" sweep:** close the prompt with *"nothing visible that was manufactured after [year] — cars, phones, packaging, clothing, signage all period-correct."* FIELD-VALIDATED as high-leverage: a six-layer era prompt landed near-perfect first pass (period car, payphone, power lines, smog sky, wardrobe, even a period license plate). Three named period props + this sweep is the reliable recipe.

# Nano Banana — graphic & illustration styles (non-photoreal)

The rest of this skill is photoreal-heavy. This covers the *other* half of Nano Banana Pro's commercial use: illustration, vector-look, 3D, anime, logos, brand-identity systems, and accurate diagrams. Name the style register precisely — "illustration" alone averages to mush.

## Style-family register blocks

| Style | Cue block |
|---|---|
| Flat vector | flat fills, no gradients, bold shapes, limited palette, clean geometry, generous negative space |
| Line art | single-weight or tapered ink lines, no fill or one flat fill, confident contours |
| 3D render | soft global illumination, subsurface on characters, matte clay or glossy plastic, gentle DOF (clay-render / Octane / Pixar-soft) |
| Anime / manga | cel shading, flat skin with hard shadow shapes, screentone (manga = B&W halftone), expressive eyes |
| Comic / halftone | inked outlines, Ben-Day dots, limited spot colour, panel energy |
| Watercolour / gouache | visible paper tooth, bleeding edges, granulation, soft layered washes |
| Isometric | true isometric (no perspective convergence), neat 2:1 grid, miniature-diorama clarity |
| Pixel art | hard pixel grid, limited palette, dithering, no anti-alias |
| Sticker / die-cut | thick white border, drop shadow, glossy, bold subject on transparent/flat ground |
| Risograph | 2–3 spot inks, mis-registration, grainy overprint, fluoro pink/blue |
| Low-poly | faceted triangulated forms, flat-shaded facets, gradient lighting |
| Paper-cut / collage | layered cut-paper depth, soft shadows between layers, torn or clean edges |
| Technical / blueprint | white line on blueprint blue, callouts, measured, schematic |

Pick ONE register; pair with subject + palette + one light idea. Mixing two registers cancels both (same rule as director/photographer blocks).

## Logos & brand-identity systems

- **Logo construction:** name the type — wordmark, lettermark, pictorial mark, abstract mark, combination, emblem. Spec the idea, geometry, and weight: *"a combination mark — a minimal line-drawn fox head above the wordmark 'NORTHERLY' in a bold geometric grotesque, even optical spacing, single ink colour."* Quote the wordmark text (`references/text_rendering.md`).
- **Identity-system workflow (Nano Banana Pro):** generate the mark first, then build the system **one mockup at a time** — *"now apply this logo to: business card, tote, signage, app icon, packaging, billboard, bus-stop ad — generate one at a time, 16:9 each, consistent brand styling."* Pro drapes the mark onto surfaces with correct curvature and lighting.
- **Caveat — no true vector:** Nano Banana outputs **raster, not editable vector**. For production logos, treat the render as a *concept/comp*, then redraw/trace as real vector; render at 2K+ so edges are clean. Exact final wordmarks finish best in a vector tool.

## Accurate diagrams & infographics

Nano Banana Pro can reason and render labelled diagrams (the chai-recipe register). To keep them correct:
- **Supply every label and number** — never let the model invent data; it renders plausible-but-wrong figures.
- State a factual constraint: *"a scientifically accurate cross-section,"* *"historically accurate [era],"* and **verify the result** before use.
- 3–5 labelled steps/segments, consistent icon style, restrained palette, legible labels, **render at 2K+** (small labels blur at 1K).
- For social infographic/data *posts*, see `references/creative_posts_copy_type_layout.md` §5.
